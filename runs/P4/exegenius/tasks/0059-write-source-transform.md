## This page

Concept: Source transform procedure
Gloss: The computed transform of a prime-axes source array: expand axes by A, apply the chirp transform on power-of-two axes, compress with B₀, and scale by 2^γ; its exact counterpart is R F_s, and the opposite-sign version is obtained by conjugation.

## This page in the argument

The step: The computed transform of a source array on the prime axes expands the axes by $\mathcal A$, applies the chirp transform on the power-of-two axes, compresses with $\mathcal B_0$ and scales by $2^\gamma$; its exact counterpart is $RF_{\boldsymbol s}$, and the opposite-sign version is obtained by conjugation.
How: Compose the linewise resampling expansion, the chirp transform and the linewise compression, and read off the exact counterpart from the tensor resampling identity.
Why: The tensor identity writes the source transform as a compression, the middle transform and an expansion, so the procedure applies those three factors in order. (the sources leave this for the reader to work out: spell it out)

Its sub-steps, in order:

- The procedure applies the linewise approximation to $\mathcal A$, the computed chirp transform, the linewise approximation to $\mathcal B_0$, and multiplies the numerators exactly by $2^\gamma$.
- Conjugating the input and output of the procedure gives the opposite-sign transform, whose exact counterpart is $RF_{\boldsymbol s}^+$.

It serves these steps, from the main result down:

- A single deterministic fixed-tape Turing machine multiplies two $n$-bit integers exactly in $O(n(\lg n)^{1-\kappa})$ time with $\kappa=2^{-182}$.; how: Reduce multiplication to convolution over $\mathbb C[y]/(y^r+1)$ through radix digits, Chinese-remainder axes, Gaussian resampling and chirps, speed up the data movement and the butterfly arithmetic with two network-based tape procedures with a strict rank saving, recover the exact product by rounding and carries, and add up the costs. (on [Integer multiplication in O(n (lg n)^(1−κ)) time](main-theorem.md))
- Convert the inputs to radix-$2^b$ digits, map them to the prime axes by the CRT map, compute the two source transforms, multiply pointwise, compute the opposite transform, rescale by $S$ and $2^{2b+4}$, round, undo the CRT map and propagate carries to output the exact $2n$-bit product.; how: Instantiate the earlier procedures with explicit compatible parameters and chain them in the order digits, CRT map, source transforms, pointwise product, opposite transform, rescaling, rounding, inverse map, carries. The source arrays are stored in the padded power-of-two box, with zeros outside the valid source coordinates. The compressed scalar output uses $w_{\rm c}$-bit components. Appending $\gamma$ zero bits to each word gives the exact scaled numerator in the known signed width $w_{\rm c}+\gamma$. For the stated input norm, the disk estimate below proves that every scaled coordinate still belongs to $\mathbb D_p$. For the same reason, never stop processes by name or pattern (`pkill`, `killall`), which would stop other tasks' commands too: stop a command of your own by its process id.
