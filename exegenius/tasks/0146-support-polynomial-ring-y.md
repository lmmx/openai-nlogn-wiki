## Quotations

### 1

Claim: Hence multiplication by a power of $y$ is a signed cyclic shift of the list

Quotation, from Integer multiplication below n log n (OpenAI, 2026): "multiplication by such a root is a signed shift of the coefficient list"

Paragraph:

> Multiplying by $y$ moves each entry up one place, and the entry at degree $r-1$ wraps to degree $0$ with a minus sign, as in the example. Since $y^k=-y^{k-r}$ for $r\le k<2r$ and $y^{2r}=1$, every power of $y$ acts this way some number of times. Hence multiplication by a power of $y$ is a signed cyclic shift of the list.
