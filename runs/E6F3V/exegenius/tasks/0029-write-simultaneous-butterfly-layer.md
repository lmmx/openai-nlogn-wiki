## This page

Concept: Simultaneous butterfly layer
Gloss: Applying H₀ to one selected bit in each of D address chunks at once, on arrays of ring records, in O(V d^{λ′}) with λ′ < 1 instead of D whole-array passes, via the recursive complex network on packed bit changes.

## This page in the argument

The step: Applying $H_0(u,v)=((u+v)/2,(u-v)/2)$ to one selected bit in each of $D\le d$ address chunks of $K$ bits, on records of $r$ complex coefficients, costs $O(Vd^{\lambda'})$ for a fixed $\lambda'<1$, instead of $D$ whole-array passes, and is exact up to one final truncation.
How: Rerun the rank-saving recursion with the complex phase network: express the tensor product of $H_0$ through the kernel $C$, whose frame change on an edge is one translation kernel per residual basis vector, so each edge becomes smaller layers on slots of fewer chunks, with the binary basis changes done by packed whole-chunk moves.
Why: Phase frames express a tensor product of the kernel $C$ through smaller groups of selected bits separated by binary basis changes, which are implemented by moving whole address chunks and splitting rows among the wire roles to shrink each recursive call. (the sources say so: "Its phase
frames express a $C$ tensor product using smaller groups of
selected bits, separated by binary changes of basis. We implement those
changes by moving whole address chunks. Splitting complete rows among
the network's wire roles then reduces the volume of each recursive
call. Finally, we bound the intermediate dyadic values and convert the
completed $C$ layer to the normalized $H_0$ layer. The entire layer is
evaluated exactly before one final truncation." (paper))
Striking feature: The cost is $O(Vd^{\lambda'})$ for $d$ axes at once, strictly sublinear in the number of axes; it comes from the same strict inequality $s_{\rm c}/W_{\rm c}<m^\sigma$ that saves a power in address interchange. (explain why it holds, from the method)

Its sub-steps, in order:

- With frames diagonalized by Hadamard matrices and phases $i^{\mathrm{wt}}$, the change across any edge is a product of translation kernels $C_v=aI+bX_v$, one per orthonormal basis vector of its residual, and $H_0^{\otimes D}=b^D\mathcal S_DC^{\otimes D}\mathcal S_D$. (on [Phase frames and the two-point kernel C](phase-frames.md))
- Given chunks $x,y,z$ of $L=fK$ bits, the $f$ XORs $y_{j_i}\leftarrow y_{j_i}\oplus x_{j_i}$ at positions $j_i=\rho+iK$ are done by constantly many cyclic shifts of whole chunk ranges (restoring $z$) plus a deterministic repair of the exceptional addresses, a fraction at most $80(f-1)2^{-K}$, in $O(V(L^\tau+1))$ time. (on [Packed selected-bit addition](packed-selected-bit-addition.md))
- For $e$ active chunks one network run makes $s$ calls on slots of $e/m$ chunks, each on a stream of volume $V/W$, so the cost per unit volume satisfies $F(e)\le(s/W)F(e/m)+O((eK)^\tau+1)$, which unrolls to $O(d^{\lambda'})$ for $d$ axes with $\lambda'<1$.
- Intermediate values in a layer on records of $r$ coefficients are exact Gaussian dyadics: reserving $p+\Delta$ fractional bits and $\Delta+3$ integer bits with $\Delta=\lceil C_0d^{C_1}\rceil=o(p)$ represents every intermediate coefficient exactly, and a single truncation $Q_p$ follows the whole contractive layer. (on [Exact dyadic intermediates with one truncation](guard-bits.md))
- The row construction and the recurrence analysis give total time $O(Vd^{\lambda'})$ on fixed tapes, including all padded and auxiliary streams, and the exact $C$-layer is converted to $\mathcal H$ by the phase identity.
- Every row of $\mathcal H$ has absolute row sum one, so $\mathcal H$ maps disk arrays to disk arrays, and componentwise truncation keeps the disk with error less than $\sqrt2\,2^{-p}$.

It serves these steps, from the main result down:

- One deterministic multitape Turing machine multiplies $n$-bit integers exactly in $O(n(\lg n)^{1-\kappa})$ time with $\kappa=2^{-182}$, refuting the $n\log n$ optimality conjecture in this model.; how: Reduce the product to a convolution of short digits evaluated by Fourier transforms, speed up the two costly ingredients (moving data between addresses, and butterfly arithmetic) by a power of a block size using one recursive saving, and total every cost term. (on [Sub-n-log-n integer multiplication](main-theorem.md))
