Signed packing is a signed Kronecker substitution: it evaluates polynomials with signed integer coefficients at a large power of two, multiplies the resulting integers, and reads the product's coefficients back out. It gives the products in the ring $\mathcal R_r$ that convolution needs, in $O(rp\log(rp))$ time.

Let $p$ be the number of fractional bits in each coefficient component, with $r<2^p$. Take $f,g$ in the [Polynomial ring modulo y^r + 1](ring-y.md) whose coefficients are $2^{-p}$ times Gaussian integers of modulus at most 1 (see [Fixed-point Gaussian dyadic records](fixed-point.md)). Write $f=2^{-p}(a+ib)$ and $g=2^{-p}(c+ie)$, where $a,b,c,e$ are integer-coefficient polynomials of degree below $r$ with coefficients of absolute value at most $2^p$. Then $fg=2^{-2p}\bigl((ac-be)+i(ae+bc)\bigr)$, so four integer polynomial products suffice.

Set $B_0=2^{4p}$, the packing base, and for $u\in\{a,b,c,e\}$ let $A_u=u(B_0)=\sum_{j=0}^{r-1}u_jB_0^j$ be the signed evaluation integer. Since $|u_j|\le2^p$ and $2^{p+1}<B_0-1$, the geometric sum gives $|A_u|\le2^p(B_0^r-1)/(B_0-1)<B_0^r/2$, so its magnitude fits in $4pr$ bits.

To form $A_u$, start with carry $\gamma_0=0$, let $d_j$ be the residue of $u_j+\gamma_j$ modulo $B_0$ with $0\le d_j<B_0$, and put $\gamma_{j+1}=(u_j+\gamma_j-d_j)/B_0$. Since $-B_0<u_j+\gamma_j<B_0$ whenever $\gamma_j\in\{-1,0\}$, the carry stays in $\{-1,0\}$. Telescoping gives $A_u=\sum_{j<r}d_jB_0^j+\gamma_rB_0^r$, so the blocks $d_j$ are a fixed-width two's-complement encoding of $A_u$, negative exactly when $\gamma_r=-1$.

Signed packing computes its integer products by the [Established O(n log n) multiplier](hvdh-multiplier.md), used only as an ordinary subroutine. Multiplying the four pairs of absolute values and restoring each sign gives $A_aA_c,A_bA_e,A_aA_e,A_bA_c$; for $h=ac$ in $\mathbb Z[y]$, $A_aA_c=(ac)(B_0)=\sum_{j=0}^{2r-2}h_jB_0^j$, and the other three have the same form.

Each $h_j$ sums at most $r$ products of coefficients of size at most $2^p$, so $|h_j|\le r2^{2p}<2^{3p}<B_0/2$. Hence after the lower coefficients are removed, the next is identified by its centered residue modulo $B_0$, the unique integer in $[-B_0/2,B_0/2)$ congruent to it <cite data-source="paper">this strict bound identifies the next coefficient by its centered residue modulo $B_0$</cite>.

For extraction, let $Z$ be a signed product integer. Since $|Z|<B_0^{2r}/4$, write $Z=\sum_{j<2r}q_jB_0^j+\varepsilon B_0^{2r}$ with $0\le q_j<B_0$ and $\varepsilon\in\{-1,0\}$. Put $\rho_0=0$. Then $\widehat h_j$ is the unique integer in $[-B_0/2,B_0/2)$ congruent to $q_j+\rho_j$ modulo $B_0$, with carry $\rho_{j+1}=(q_j+\rho_j-\widehat h_j)/B_0\in\{0,1\}$, because $0\le q_j+\rho_j\le B_0$. Inductively $\sum_{i<j}q_iB_0^i=\sum_{i<j}\widehat h_iB_0^i+\rho_jB_0^j$; if $\widehat h_i=h_i$ for $i<j$, comparing $Z$ modulo $B_0^{j+1}$ gives $q_j+\rho_j\equiv h_j$, so by the separation bound $\widehat h_j=h_j$.

The real and imaginary numerator streams are $ac-be$ and $ae+bc$. Signed packing reduces its numerator streams to the ring $\mathcal R_r$ by subtracting the coefficient at $j+r$ from the one at $j$ for $0\le j<r$, since $y^{j+r}=-y^j$. Let $H_j$ be the resulting Gaussian integer coefficient of $y^j$. For each $j_1\in\{0,\dots,r-1\}$ exactly one $j_2$ in that range has $j_1+j_2\equiv j\pmod r$, with sign $+$ if $j_1+j_2=j$ and $-$ if $j_1+j_2=j+r$. So $H_j$ is a sum of exactly $r$ signed products of coefficients of modulus at most $2^p$, hence $|H_j|\le r2^{2p}$, and the coefficients $2^{-2p}H_j/r$ of $fg/r$ have modulus at most 1: the normalized product of disk polynomials lies in the disk.

Truncating $2^{-2p}H_j/r$ to a multiple of $2^{-p}$ is a right shift by $p+\ell$ bits, $\ell=\log_2r$, at $O(p)$ per coefficient. Four exact integer products dominate the linear packing, extraction, combination, wrapping and truncation costs, so the total is $O(rp\log(rp))$ <cite data-source="paper">in time $O(rp\log(rp))$ on a fixed number of tapes</cite>, with error less than $\sqrt2\,2^{-p}$ from $fg/r$ <cite data-source="paper">with error less than $\sqrt2\,2^{-p}$ from $fg/r$</cite>.
