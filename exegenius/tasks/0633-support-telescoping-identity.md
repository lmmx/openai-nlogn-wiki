### 1

Claim: The paper states this as

Quotation, from Integer multiplication below n log n (OpenAI, 2026): "The signed sum of dimension changes telescopes at every gate"

Paragraph:

> In a network, a wire runs from a source vertex, through gate vertices where wires are combined, to a sink vertex. Applied to a wire, the list $\iota_0,\dots,\iota_o$ is the dimension of the wire at its source, then at each gate it passes, and finally at its sink. At a gate, a wire has one incoming and one outgoing incidence with the same label, so the two changes there cancel. Only the source and sink dimensions remain. The paper states this as. Summing over all wires gives the sum of sink dimensions minus the sum of source dimensions, which is what the paper needs.
