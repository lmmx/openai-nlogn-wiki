## This page

Concept: Packed selected-bit addition
Gloss: XORing the selected bits of one address chunk into the corresponding bits of another using eight packed cyclic shifts guarded by a third chunk, then deterministically repairing the small set of addresses where guards overflow.
Also called: binary change of basis, deterministic repair, guard values

## This page in the argument

The step: For three $L$-bit chunks $x,y,z$ with $L=fK$, the XORs $y_{j_i}\gets y_{j_i}\oplus x_{j_i}$ at positions $j_i=\rho+iK$ are performed while restoring $z$, in time $O(V(L^\tau+1))$ under the layer's parameter growth.
How: Use eight packed cyclic shifts of whole chunk ranges, each controlled by $x$ and guarded by parities of $z$, which flip the selected parities segment by segment, then correct the small address-determined set where guards overflow by extracting and radix-sorting those records.
Why: The correction depends only on addresses, so the time bound is worst-case over all payloads; and the exceptional set is so small that sorting it costs $o(V)$. (the sources say so: "The correction depends only on addresses, so the time bound is
worst-case over all payloads." (paper))

Its sub-steps, in order:

- The eight conditional updates flip the parity of $u$ exactly when $c=1$, preserve both quotients by two and restore the auxiliary integer $w$.
- By the union bound over the $2(f-1)$ guards, the exceptional set satisfies $|\mathcal B|\le\delta_{\rm bad}M$ with $\delta_{\rm bad}=\min\{1,80(f-1)2^{-K}\}$.
- The packed shifts $S_0$ and the ideal map $T_0$ agree off $\mathcal B$, so $T_0S_0^{-1}$ fixes the complement of $\mathcal B$ and permutes $\mathcal B$.
- Extracting the exceptional records with destination ranks, radix-sorting them and reinserting them implements $T_0$ everywhere.

It serves these steps, from the main result down:

- One deterministic fixed-alphabet, fixed-tape Turing machine computes the exact $2n$-bit product of two $n$-bit integers in $O(n(\lg n)^{1-\kappa})$ worst-case time with $\kappa=2^{-182}$, disproving the $n\log n$ conjecture in this model.; how: Reduce the product to radix-digit convolution, move it by a Chinese-remainder map, Gaussian resampling, chirps and a phase twist to convolution over $\mathbb C[y]/(y^r+1)$ computed by synthetic transforms; speed up the transforms' address movement and butterfly layers with two network-based tape procedures; then prove exact recovery and total the costs. (on [Integer multiplication in O(n (lg n)^{1−κ}) time](main-theorem.md))
- Two tape procedures save over plain scanning: address chunks of width $u$ are interchanged in $O(Vu^\tau)$ time, and a simultaneous normalized butterfly layer on $D\le d$ chunks costs $O(Vd^{\lambda'})$, with fixed $\tau,\lambda'<1$.; how: Build each procedure from its own fixed linear network that exchanges two banks and restores scratch wires, apply the network pointwise to row-split role streams with frames chosen so every edge is a few smaller calls, and solve the volume-normalized recurrence.
- The simultaneous butterfly layer: on an array of polynomial records with $D\le d$ consecutive $K$-bit chunks, $H_0$ applied to one common bit position of every chunk, followed by truncation $Q_p$, is computed in $O(Vd^{\lambda'})$ time with error below $\sqrt2\,2^{-p}$.; how: Write $H_0^{\otimes D}=b^D\mathcal S_DC^{\otimes D}\mathcal S_D$, compute $C^{\otimes D}$ recursively with the complex network, keep every intermediate value exact in a guarded fixed-point format, and truncate once at the end. (on [Simultaneous butterfly layer](simultaneous-layer.md))
- The tensor product $C^{\otimes D}$ of the kernel $C$ on the selected bits of $D\le d$ chunks is computed exactly in $O(Vd^{\lambda'})$ time, by recursion in which a group of $e$ selected axes uses $s_{\rm c}$ children on $e/m$ axes, each on a stream of volume $V/W$.; how: Run the complex network on row-split role streams with column phase frames, implement each edge's frame change by a binary change of basis (packed row additions) and one recursive $C^{\otimes e/m}$ call per residual vector, and unroll the volume-normalized recurrence. (on [Recursive C-layer via phase frames](recursive-c-layer.md))

**S3.1.6.1**: The eight conditional updates flip the parity of $u$ exactly when $c=1$, preserve both quotients by two and restore the auxiliary integer $w$.
Source: "Thus the parity of $u$
is flipped precisely when $c=1$, both quotients under division by two are

> operations do nothing. If $c=1$, the first four operations induce the cycle $(00\ 01\ 10)$ in every square \[ \{2a,2a+1\}\times\{2b,2b+1\},\qquad a,b\in\mathbb Z, \] where the corner labels record the parities of $(u,w)$. The last four induce $(01\ 10\ 11)$. Applying the first cycle and then the second gives $(00\ 10)(01\ 11)$. These facts follow by substituting the four parity pairs into the displayed operations; translation by $(2a,2b)$ does not change any predicate. Consequently the complete output is \begin{equation} \label{eq:eight-packed-result} (u',w')= \bigl(u+c(1-2(u\bmod2)),\ w\bigr). \end{equation} Here $u\bmod2\in\{0,1\}$, also for negative $u$. Thus the parity of $u$ is flipped precisely when $c=1$, both quotients under division by two are preserved, and the arbitrary auxiliary integer $w$ is restored. Each coordinate is updated four times by at most one, so its displacement from its initial value is at most four, even if an intermediate point leaves its starting quotient square. The weaker bound eight will suffice below.

**S3.1.6.3**: The packed shifts $S_0$ and the ideal map $T_0$ agree off $\mathcal B$, so $T_0S_0^{-1}$ fixes the complement of $\mathcal B$ and permutes $\mathcal B$.
Source: "Thus $T_0S_0^{-1}$ fixes $\mathcal B^c$ and permutes $\mathcal B$." (paper)
> The ideal map $T_0$ preserves all guard values and therefore preserves $\mathcal B$. Both $T_0$ and $S_0$ are bijections, and they agree off $\mathcal B$. Taking complements in the finite rectangle gives \[ S_0(\mathcal B^c)=T_0(\mathcal B^c)=\mathcal B^c, \qquad S_0(\mathcal B)=\mathcal B. \] Thus $T_0S_0^{-1}$ fixes $\mathcal B^c$ and permutes $\mathcal B$. It remains to implement the rotations and this correction on tapes.

**S3.1.6.4**: Extracting the exceptional records with destination ranks, radix-sorting them and reinserting them implements $T_0$ everywhere.
Source: "This implements $T_0$ everywhere." (paper)
> If $q_h,q'_h$ are the current and destination values of chunk $h\in\{x,y,z\}$ and $\sigma_h$ is its record stride, the fixed spectator coordinates give \[ \operatorname{rank}(q')=\operatorname{rank}(q)+ \sum_{h\in\{x,y,z\}}(q'_h-q_h)\sigma_h. \] The differences are signed. Attach this destination rank, padded to $\lceil\log_2M\rceil\le A_{\rm rect}$ bits, to the extracted record, and mark its hole on a fixed track of the full stream. Hold the full stream untouched while stable binary radix sorting scans only the extracted records and keys, least significant key bit first. Since $T_0S_0^{-1}$ permutes $\mathcal B$, these keys are distinct and are exactly the ranks of the marked holes. One forward full-stream scan reinserts the sorted records. This implements $T_0$ everywhere.
