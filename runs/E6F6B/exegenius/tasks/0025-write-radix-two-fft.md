## This page

Concept: Radix-two FFT butterflies
Gloss: The decimation-in-frequency recursion that pairs entries k and k+t/2, applies the two-point butterfly H₀ = ½[[1,1],[1,−1]] and a twiddle multiplication, then recurses on both halves, leaving outputs in bit-reversed order.

## Where the argument relies on this page

- Applying $H_0(u,v)=((u+v)/2,(u-v)/2)$ to the same bit position in $D\le d$ address chunks of $K$ bits at once, on polynomial records with fixed-point complex coefficients, costs $O(Vd^{\lambda'})$ for a fixed $\lambda'<1$ and ends with one truncation of error below $\sqrt2\,2^{-p}$. (on [Simultaneous butterfly layer](simultaneous-butterfly-layer.md))
- Fourier-type transforms over $\mathbb C[y]/(y^r+1)$ use $\omega_t=y^{2r/t}$ as root of unity, so twiddle multiplications are signed coefficient shifts, radix-two butterflies leave outputs in bit-reversed order, and the forward and opposite versions invert each other up to $1/t$. (on [Synthetic polynomial transforms](synthetic-transforms.md))
- A positional address permutation places the selected bits of all participating axes at a common offset in consecutive $K$-bit chunks, so each FFT round is one simultaneous layer, at total cost $O(Tp[dK+pK^{\tau-1}+\ell d^{\lambda'}])$ for $M=T/r$ records. (on [Synthetic transform layout on tape](synthetic-transform-layout.md))

## Evidence from the sources, with the paragraph around each quote

- "decimation-in-frequency step pairs $z_k$ with $z_{k+t/2}$" (paper)

  > Write \[ H_0=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}. \] The length-one procedure is the identity. For $t\geq2$, the decimation-in-frequency step pairs $z_k$ with $z_{k+t/2}$ for $0\leq k<t/2$. The two outputs of $H_0$ occupy these same slots, whose high address bit is $b=0$ or $b=1$, respectively. Multiply the $b=1$ output by $\omega_t^{-k}$, and then apply the length-$t/2$ procedure separately within the two branches. This computes $F_t^-$ because, for $b\in\{0,1\}$, \begin{equation} (F_t^-z)_{2h+b} =\frac1{t/2}\sum_{k=0}^{t/2-1} (\omega_t^2)^{-hk}\omega_t^{-bk} \frac{z_k+(-1)^b z_{k+t/2}}2. \label{eq:synthetic-dif} \end{equation} To follow the recursion, name the original address-bit slots of an axis of length $t=2^a$ by $a-1,\ldots,0$, from most to least significant. For $0\leq s\leq a$, after $s$ levels the values of the first $s$ named slots are branch bit

- "the entry $(F_t^-z)_j$ occupies slot $\operatorname{rev}_a(j)$" (paper)

  > After all $a$ rounds, the named slots from high to low therefore contain the frequency bits from low to high. If $\operatorname{rev}_a(j)$ reverses the $a$ binary digits of $j$, the entry $(F_t^-z)_j$ occupies slot $\operatorname{rev}_a(j)$. Let $B_t$ denote this record permutation: it moves the entry at $j$ to $\operatorname{rev}_a(j)$. The recursion writes its outputs directly in this order; it does not move whole frequency indices.
