Error tracking and exact recovery shows that the computed convolution coefficients lie within $1/2$ of the true integers, so rounding recovers them and carry propagation gives the exact binary product. Every stage is a contraction, so errors add.

The computation runs on [Fixed-point Gaussian dyadic records](fixed-point.md) with $p=6b$ fractional bits, where $b=\lceil\log_2n\rceil$ is the digit width. Write $h_p=2^{-p}$ for the grid spacing, use sup norms throughout, and let an error of $a$ mean absolute error below $ah_p$. Put $L=\log_2T$; this $L$ is not the linear map $L$ of the contractions page. Computed arrays stay in the unit disk (norm at most $1$); for large $n$, $S\le T<2^b$ and $L<b$. Each [synthetic transform](synthetic-transform.md) has error less than $\sqrt2\ell\,2^{-p}<L2^{-p}$ for all large $n$ <cite data-source="paper">$\sqrt2\ell\,2^{-p}<L2^{-p}$</cite>.

Errors add through the [Contractions and bilinear contractions](contraction-maps.md) making up each transform and product. A bilinear contraction $\mathcal M$ has $\|\mathcal M(f,g)\|\le\|f\|\|g\|$, and $\mathcal M(f',g')-\mathcal M(f,g)=\mathcal M(f'-f,g')+\mathcal M(f,g'-g)$ by bilinearity. Hence $\|\mathcal M(f',g')-\mathcal M(f,g)\|\le\|f'-f\|\|g'\|+\|f\|\|g'-g\|$. For operands in the disk, $\|g'\|\le1$ and $\|f\|\le1$, so the two input errors add.

A phase multiplication computes $Q_p(\widetilde ex)$ for $ex$, where $e$ is a unit phase, $\widetilde e$ a grid approximation with $|\widetilde e-e|<2h_p$, and $x$ an array in the disk. The approximation moves the product by under $2\|x\|h_p$ and truncation by under $\sqrt2h_p$, so $\|Q_p(\widetilde ex)-ex\|<(2\|x\|+\sqrt2)h_p<4h_p$. Also $\|Q_p(\widetilde ex)\|\le\|x\|$, since $|\widetilde e|\le1$.

The ring convolution $\mathcal M_r$ of [Phase twist](phase-twist.md) uses two forward synthetic transforms, a pointwise product divided by $r$ and one opposite transform, so before its exact multiplication by $M=T/r$ it errs by under $3L+2$. The returned convolution $(f*g)/T$ has error at most $E_{\rm ring}:=M(3L+2)<5TL$, and if one operand has norm at most $.7$ the output norm is below $.7+5TLh_p<.701$ <cite data-source="paper">E_{\rm ring}:=M(3L+2)<5TL.</cite> The computed complex convolution $\widetilde{\mathcal M}_{\mathbb C}$ twists the inputs, applies $\mathcal M_r$ and untwists the output, so the errors of two twists (each under $4$), the ring step and the untwist (under $4$) add: $\|\widetilde{\mathcal M}_{\mathbb C}(f,g)-\mathcal M_{\mathbb C}(f,g)\|<(E_{\rm ring}+4+4+4)h_p<6TLh_p$.

The computed chirp transform $\widetilde{\mathscr C}$ is built from the [Bluestein chirp identity](chirp.md): a phase product, a complex convolution and a last phase product. With $\boldsymbol t$ the axis lengths, $F_{\boldsymbol t}$ the exact transform and $Q$ the target permutation, the errors $2$ (kernel approximation), $4$, $6TL$ and $4$ give $\|\widetilde{\mathscr C}(x)-QF_{\boldsymbol t}x\|<(6TL+2+4+4)h_p<8TLh_p$.

The computed source transform $\widetilde{\mathscr F}$ applies the expansion $\mathcal A$ of [Gaussian resampling](gaussian-resampling.md), then $\widetilde{\mathscr C}$, then the compression $\mathcal B_0$, then an exact scaling by $2^\gamma$. Expansion and compression add under $2dp^2$, so the error before scaling is under $2dp^2+8TL$. As $2dp^2<TL$ eventually, scaling gives error less than $E_sh_p$ with $E_s=9\cdot2^\gamma TL$ <cite data-source="paper">E_s=9\,2^\gamma TL,</cite> and $b\ge2^{24}$ gives $\gamma\le b/4$ <cite data-source="paper">$b\ge2^{24}$ implies $\gamma\le b/4$</cite>. The opposite transform $\widetilde{\mathscr F}^{+}$ has the same error.

Here $u$ and $v$ denote the arrays of scaled radix digits of the two inputs, not the integers: each digit is stored as its value times $2^{-b-2}$, so entries are below $1/4$. Let $U=RF_{\boldsymbol s}u$ and $V_1=RF_{\boldsymbol s}v$ be the exact source transforms and $\widetilde U=\widetilde{\mathscr F}(u)$, $\widetilde V_1=\widetilde{\mathscr F}(v)$ the computed ones, of norm under $1/4+E_sh_p<.26$. Then $z=Q_p(\widetilde U\cdot\widetilde V_1)$ has norm under $.26^2<.5$. Since $\widetilde U\cdot\widetilde V_1-U\cdot V_1=(\widetilde U-U)\cdot\widetilde V_1+U\cdot(\widetilde V_1-V_1)$ has two terms of norm below $E_sh_p$ each and truncation adds under $2h_p$, we get $\|z-U\cdot V_1\|<(2E_s+2)h_p$.

Let $G=RF_{\boldsymbol s}^+$ be the exact opposite transform. Because the retained permutation cancels ([Retained permutation cancellation](retained-permutations.md)), $G(U\cdot V_1)=(u*v)/S^2$ <cite data-source="paper">=\frac{u*v}{S^2}.</cite> Since $\|z\|<.5$ the computed opposite transform applies to $z$, and $G$ is a contraction, so $\|\widetilde{\mathscr F}^{+}(z)-G(U\cdot V_1)\|\le\|\widetilde{\mathscr F}^{+}(z)-Gz\|+\|G(z-U\cdot V_1)\|<(3E_s+2)h_p$. Exact multiplication by $S$ makes $\widetilde w$ approximate $w=(u*v)/S$ with error less than $E_wh_p$, where $E_w:=S(3E_s+2)<28\cdot2^\gamma T^2L$, since $S\le T$.

The integer coefficients are $2^{2b+4}Sw$: $2^{2b+4}$ undoes the two digit scales and $S$ the factor $1/S$. After this second scaling the absolute error is $2^{2b+4}S\cdot28\cdot2^\gamma T^2L\cdot2^{-6b}$. Using $S\le T<2^b$ and $L<b$, it is less than $448b\,2^{-b+\gamma}$, since $2^{2b+4}ST^2<2^{5b+4}$ and $28\cdot2^4=448$. Since $\gamma\le b/4$ for $b\ge2^{24}$, it is at most $448b\,2^{-3b/4}<\tfrac12$ <cite data-source="paper">\le448b\,2^{-3b/4}<\frac12.</cite> The scaling amplifies the grid error by under $2^{5b+4}$ against $2^{6b}$ available: about $b$ spare bits, of which $2^\gamma$ spends at most $b/4$. The true coefficients are integers, so an error strictly below $1/2$ makes the nearest integer to the represented value the unique correct coefficient.

Carry propagation rebuilds the binary product from the coefficients $c_j$ of the [Multiplication as radix-digit convolution](radix-digit-reduction.md), with $c_j\le q(2^b-1)^2$ and $q\le2^b$. Put $Q_0=2^b$. For $0\le j<S$ the carries obey $k_0=0$, $d_j=(c_j+k_j)\bmod Q_0$ and $k_{j+1}=\lfloor(c_j+k_j)/Q_0\rfloor$, where $k_j$ is the carry into position $j$ and $d_j$ the $j$-th output digit. Then $0\le k_j\le q(Q_0-1)<Q_0^2$: if this holds at $j$, then $c_j+k_j\le q(Q_0-1)^2+q(Q_0-1)=qQ_0(Q_0-1)$, so $k_{j+1}\le q(Q_0-1)$. Since $c_j+k_j=d_j+Q_0k_{j+1}$, multiplying by $Q_0^j$ and adding to the invariant at $j$ carries $\sum_{h<j}c_hQ_0^h=\sum_{h<j}d_hQ_0^h+k_jQ_0^j$ from $j$ to $j+1$, and it holds at $j=0$. The unprocessed terms are multiples of $Q_0^j$, so the first $j$ radix digits are fixed. The product is less than $2^{2n}$, since both inputs are below $2^n$, so all bits above position $2n-1$ are zero and exactly $2n$ bits are output.
