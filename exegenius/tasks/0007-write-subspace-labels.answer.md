Nested subspace labels give each vertex (source, gate or sink) of the [exchange network](exchange-network.md) a subspace of an $m$-dimensional space, nested along every edge. Their dimension changes count the smaller calls an edge costs <cite data-source="paper">Their dimension changes count the smaller operations needed on an edge</cite>. They choose the frames of [Frames on wire arrays](array-frames.md) so that $s$, the total number of smaller calls, is below $Wm$, where $W$ is the number of wires.

With $h=100$ the size of the ground set whose three-element subsets (*triples*) index the network, bit labels live in $F=\mathbb Q^h$ and complex labels in $F=\mathbb F_2^h$. The bit form $\langle x,y\rangle=x^{\mathsf T}y-\frac19(\sum_ix_i)(\sum_iy_i)$ is nondegenerate <cite data-source="paper">This form has eigenvalues $1$ and $1-h/9=-91/9$, so it is nondegenerate</cite>; the complex one is the dot product. For a triple $T$, $t_T$ is its indicator vector. As $t_S^{\mathsf T}t_T=|S\cap T|$ and $\sum_i(t_T)_i=3$, $\langle t_S,t_T\rangle=|S\cap T|-1$, while $t_S\cdot t_T=|S\cap T|\bmod 2$. So $\langle t_T,t_T\rangle\in\{2,1\}$: each line $\langle t_T\rangle$ is *nondegenerate* (the form stays nondegenerate on it, so $F=\langle t_T\rangle\perp t_T^\perp$), and neighbouring triples, meeting in $1$ element (bit) or $0$ or $2$ (complex), have orthogonal indicators.

$\mathcal F=F^{\otimes3}$, of dimension $m=h^3$, is the space of $h\times h\times h$ arrays: $x\otimes y\otimes z$ has entries $x_iy_jz_k$, forms multiply factorwise, and $\dim(U\otimes V)=\dim U\dim V$. Data wires $X_a,Y_a$ are indexed by tuples $a=(a_1,a_2,a_3)$ of triples, and $N$ is the number of such tuples. With $U_a=\langle t_{a_1}\otimes t_{a_2}\otimes t_{a_3}\rangle$, the terminal labels are

$$\begin{array}{c|cc}&\text{source}&\text{sink}\\\hline X_a&U_a&\mathcal F\\ Y_a&0&U_a^\perp\\ \text{scratch}&0&\mathcal F\end{array}$$

Source dimensions sum to $N$, sink dimensions to $Nm+N(m-1)+(W-2N)m=Wm-N$. Each gate's wires carry its label on both sides, so signed changes telescope along wires to $Wm-2N$ in total. If decreasing edges lose $L$ dimensions in total, absolute changes sum to $Wm-2N+2L$, so the saving rests on keeping $L$ small.

At stage $j$, invocations vary $a_j$; put $A=F^{\otimes(j-1)}$, $P=\langle t_{a_1}\otimes\cdots\otimes t_{a_{j-1}}\rangle$, $B=P^\perp\cap A$ and $Q=\langle t_{a_{j+1}}\otimes\cdots\otimes t_{a_3}\rangle$ (empty products are scalars: $B=0$ at stage $1$). The stage moves

$$X:\ A\otimes\langle t_{a_j}\rangle\otimes Q\to A\otimes F\otimes Q,\qquad Y:\ B\otimes\langle t_{a_j}\rangle\otimes Q\to(P\otimes\langle t_{a_j}\rangle)^\perp_{A\otimes F}\otimes Q.$$

With $A'=A\otimes F$, $B'=(P\otimes\langle t_{a_j}\rangle)^\perp_{A'}$ and $Q=\langle t_{a_{j+1}}\rangle\otimes Q'$, the outputs are $A'\otimes\langle t_{a_{j+1}}\rangle\otimes Q'$ and $B'\otimes\langle t_{a_{j+1}}\rangle\otimes Q'$, stage $j+1$'s inputs; stage $1$ starts at $U_a$, $0$ and stage $3$ ends at $\mathcal F$, $U_a^\perp$.

Within a stage, with gate times in parentheses, gate labels tensored by $Q$, and $t_X,t_Y$ the indicators of a side wire's two triples:

$$\begin{aligned}\text{centre: }&0\to B\otimes F\,(1)\to A\otimes F\,(3)\to B\otimes F\,(4)\to A\otimes F\,(6)\to\mathcal F\\ \text{side: }&0\to B\otimes\langle t_Y\rangle\,(0)\to(B\otimes F)\perp(P\otimes\langle t_X\rangle)\,(2)\to(B\otimes F)\perp(P\otimes t_Y^\perp)\,(5)\to A\otimes F\,(7)\to\mathcal F\\ X:\ &A\otimes\langle t_X\rangle\to(B\otimes F)\perp(P\otimes\langle t_X\rangle)\,(2)\to A\otimes F\,(3,6,7)\\ Y:\ &B\otimes\langle t_Y\rangle\,(0)\to B\otimes F\,(1,4)\to(B\otimes F)\perp(P\otimes t_Y^\perp)\,(5)\end{aligned}$$

Splitting $A=B\perp P$ and $F=\langle t_T\rangle\perp t_T^\perp$, every arrow is a growing inclusion except centre $3\to4$, which drops $P\otimes F$, of dimension $h$; side $2\to5$ needs $t_X\perp t_Y$. With $I$ invocations of $c$ central wires each, $L=Ich$. As $I=3\binom h3^2$ and $N=\binom h3^3$, $L/N=3ch/\binom h3$, below $1/2$ for $c=100$ (bit) and $c=101$ (complex). The exact ratios are $L_{\rm b}/N=100/539$ and $L_{\rm c}/N=101/539$ <cite data-source="paper">\frac{L_{\rm b}}{N}=\frac{100}{539},\qquad \frac{L_{\rm c}}{N}=\frac{101}{539}</cite>.

In the bit network each vertex gets a rational $m\times m$ matrix: $\Pi_U$, the projection onto $U$ along $U^\perp$, at a gate or sink labelled $U$; $-\Pi_{U_a}$ at $X_a$'s source; $0$ at other sources. For $U\subset V$, $\Pi_V-\Pi_U$ is the identity on $V\cap U^\perp$ and zero on $U\perp V^\perp$, so has rank $\dim V-\dim U$, either way round. Only $X_a$'s first edge differs: its gate has label $U_a$, giving $2\Pi_{U_a}$, of rank one with no dimension change. As an edge costs one smaller call per unit of rank <cite data-source="paper">implements an edge change with one smaller interchange per unit of its rational rank</cite>, $s_{\rm b}=W_{\rm b}m-N+2L_{\rm b}<W_{\rm b}m$ <cite data-source="paper">=W_{\rm b}m-N+2L_{\rm b} =177176569088785861287000000<W_{\rm b}m</cite>. Endpoint differences are $\Pi_{U_a^\perp}+\Pi_{U_a}=\mathrm{Id}$ on $X_a$'s route and $\mathrm{Id}-0$ on the others <cite data-source="paper">the endpoint matrix difference on each routed role is exactly the shear applied to that role</cite>. The complex network has $s_{\rm c}=W_{\rm c}m-2N+2L_{\rm c}<W_{\rm c}m$ via [Binary phase frames](phase-frames.md).

The relative saving $(W_{\rm b}m-s_{\rm b})/(W_{\rm b}m)$ is only $339/22587335000000\approx1.5\cdot10^{-11}$ <cite data-source="paper">=\frac{339}{22587335000000}</cite>: positive as $L_{\rm b}/N<1/2$, tiny as at most $N$ is saved while each side wire alone costs $m$ and $W_{\rm b}m/N\approx4\cdot10^{10}$.
