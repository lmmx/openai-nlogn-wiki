## This page

Concept: Nested subspace labels and rank budget
Gloss: An assignment of nondegenerate subspaces of the m-dimensional space F^{⊗3} to the gates and terminals of the network, nested along every edge, whose total dimension change s is strictly less than the number of wires times m.
Also called: labels

## This page in the argument

The step: Every edge joins comparable nondegenerate labels in the $m$-dimensional space $F^{\otimes3}$, the only decreasing edges are central-wire returns of loss $h$, and the total absolute dimension change is $Wm-2N+2L$ with $L<N/2$.

It serves these steps, from the main result down:

- One deterministic fixed-alphabet multitape Turing machine computes the exact product of two $n$-bit integers in $O(n(\lg n)^{1-\kappa})$ worst-case time with $\kappa=2^{-182}$, disproving the $n\log n$ optimality conjecture in that model.; how: build two sublinear-per-level tape procedures from finite linear networks with a rank deficit, then chain the known reductions from integer product to synthetic polynomial transforms and bound every cost (on [Integer multiplication below n log n](main-theorem.md))
- Interchanging $u$-bit address chunks costs $O(Vu^\tau)$ and a simultaneous butterfly layer on $d$ chunks costs $O(Vd^{\lambda'})$, with $\tau,\lambda'<1$.; how: supply linear elementary scans, build two finite networks with a rank deficit, and turn each network into a volume-normalized recursion on tape
- Two fixed linear networks with $W$ wires exchange two banks of values, restore every scratch value, and admit nested subspace labels whose total edge rank $s$ satisfies $s<Wm$.; how: build a three-stage scalar bank exchange, verify that frames commute with pointwise gates, attach nested subspace labels from triple-intersection pairings, and read off the rational and binary interfaces
