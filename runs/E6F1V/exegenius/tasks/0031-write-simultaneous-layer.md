Gloss: Applies the two-point butterfly H₀ at one bit position in each of D address chunks at once in O(V d^{λ'}) time, by recursing on the complex phase network with packed basis changes, exact Gaussian-dyadic arithmetic and one final truncation.

The symbols in this page's heading denote (say so on the page):

## This page in the argument

The step: Applying $H_0=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}$ at one bit position in each of $D\le d$ equal-width address chunks costs $O(Vd^{\lambda'})$ for a fixed $\lambda'<1$, with one final truncation.
How: Rewrite $H_0^{\otimes D}$ through the phase kernel $C$, evaluate $C^{\otimes D}$ by the complex phase network whose edge calls are smaller layers on $1/W$-volume row streams, and implement the binary basis changes by packed address-bit additions.
Why: Separate butterfly factors would require $D$ whole-array passes, one per axis, whereas one network call handles many axes together. (the sources say so: "factors would require $D$ whole-array passes." (paper))

Its sub-steps, in order:

- The complex network with binary labels factors each edge's phase-frame change into $s_{\rm c}$ translation kernels $aI+bX_v$ in total, with $s_{\rm c}<W_{\rm c}m$, and yields $C^{\otimes m}$ on every role. (on [Complex phase network](phase-network.md))
- Applying the network to a group of $e=mf$ selected axes gives exactly $s_{\rm c}$ child calls, each on the $f=e/m$ axes of one slot.
- The normalized layer is recovered from the $C$ layer by diagonal phases: $H_0^{\otimes D}=b^D\mathcal S_D C^{\otimes D}\mathcal S_D$.
- The XOR $y\leftarrow y\oplus x$ on the same bit position of $f$ chunks at once, restoring a third scratch chunk, takes $O(V(L^\tau+1))$ time using eight cyclic shifts of whole chunk ranges plus a deterministic repair. (on [Packed selected-bit addition with deterministic repair](packed-bit-addition.md))
- A row index $u=Wg+w$ sends each whole row to role $w$, so every child call acts on a stream of exactly $1/W$ of the parent's volume.
- The normalized cost satisfies $F(e)\le (s/W)F(e/m)+O((eK)^\tau+1)$, with leaves of fewer than $d^{\beta}$ chunks handled by individual kernels.
- Summing the internal costs, the leaves and the base-$m$ groups gives total time $O(Vd^{\lambda'})$ on fixed tapes.
