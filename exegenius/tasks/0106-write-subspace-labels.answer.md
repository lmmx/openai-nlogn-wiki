Nested subspace labels attach to every source, gate and sink of the [exchange network](exchange-network.md) a subspace of one $m$-dimensional space, nested along every edge, so that dimension changes count the smaller calls each edge costs. They choose the frames of [Frames on wire arrays](array-frames.md) so that a network of $W$ wires makes $s<Wm$ smaller calls in total.

A subspace is *nondegenerate* when none of its nonzero vectors is orthogonal to all of it; then the space splits as $U\perp U^\perp$. *Triples* are three-element subsets of $[h]$. Bit labels live in $F=\mathbb Q^h$ with the form $\langle x,y\rangle=x^{\mathsf T}y-\frac19(\sum_ix_i)(\sum_iy_i)$, which is nondegenerate <cite data-source="paper">This form has eigenvalues $1$ and $1-h/9=-91/9$, so it is nondegenerate</cite>; complex labels live in $F=\mathbb F_2^h$ with the dot product. For two triples, named $S$ and $T$ here, the indicator vectors $t_S,t_T$ satisfy $t_S^{\mathsf T}t_T=|S\cap T|$ and have entry sum $3$, so $\langle t_S,t_T\rangle=|S\cap T|-1$; over $\mathbb F_2$, $t_S\cdot t_T=|S\cap T|\bmod 2$. Hence $\langle t_T,t_T\rangle\neq0$, so each line $\langle t_T\rangle$ is nondegenerate and $F=\langle t_T\rangle\perp t_T^\perp$, while neighbouring triples (sharing one element in the bit network, zero or two in the complex one) have orthogonal indicators.

All labels are built as [tensor products of subspaces](tensor-product-of-spaces.md) inside $\mathcal F=F^{\otimes3}$, of dimension $m=h^3$, where the form multiplies factorwise and $\dim(U\otimes V)=\dim U\dim V$. The data wires form two banks, $X$ and $Y$, whose wires $X_a,Y_a$ are indexed by the $N$ tuples $a=(a_1,a_2,a_3)$ of triples. With $U_a=\langle t_{a_1}\otimes t_{a_2}\otimes t_{a_3}\rangle$ the terminal labels are

$$
\begin{array}{c|cc}&\text{source}&\text{sink}\\\hline X_a&U_a&\mathcal F\\ Y_a&0&U_a^\perp\\ \text{scratch}&0&\mathcal F\end{array}
$$

Source dimensions total $N$ and sink dimensions $Nm+N(m-1)+(W-2N)m=Wm-N$. Every wire enters and leaves a gate with the gate's label, so along each wire the signed changes telescope to sink minus source dimension, and over all wires they sum to $Wm-2N$. If decreasing edges lose $L$ dimensions in all, absolute changes total $Wm-2N+2L$, so the labels confine decreases to few edges.

Stage $j$ runs the invocations that act on coordinate $a_j$. Within one, name locally $A=F^{\otimes(j-1)}$, the line $P=\langle t_{a_1}\otimes\cdots\otimes t_{a_{j-1}}\rangle$, its complement $B=P^\perp\cap A$, and $K=\langle t_{a_{j+1}}\otimes\cdots\otimes t_{a_3}\rangle$; empty products are scalars, so $B=0$ at stage $1$. Data labels move

$$
X:\ A\otimes\langle t_{a_j}\rangle\otimes K\to A\otimes F\otimes K,\qquad Y:\ B\otimes\langle t_{a_j}\rangle\otimes K\to\bigl((B\otimes F)\perp(P\otimes t_{a_j}^\perp)\bigr)\otimes K.
$$

Splitting $A=B\perp P$ and $F=\langle t_{a_j}\rangle\perp t_{a_j}^\perp$ cuts $A\otimes F$ into four mutually orthogonal nondegenerate pieces, since the form multiplies factorwise; the complement of the piece $P\otimes\langle t_{a_j}\rangle$ is the sum of the other three, so $(B\otimes F)\perp(P\otimes t_{a_j}^\perp)=(P\otimes\langle t_{a_j}\rangle)^\perp_{A\otimes F}$. That is the next stage's $B'$, as its $A$ and $P$ are $A'=A\otimes F$ and $P\otimes\langle t_{a_j}\rangle$. Writing $K=\langle t_{a_{j+1}}\rangle\otimes K'$, the outputs $A'\otimes\langle t_{a_{j+1}}\rangle\otimes K'$ and $B'\otimes\langle t_{a_{j+1}}\rangle\otimes K'$ are exactly stage $j+1$'s inputs. Stage $1$ starts at $U_a$ and $0$; stage $3$ ends at $\mathcal F$ and $U_a^\perp$, matching the terminals.

An invocation's gates act at times $0,\dots,7$, each joining one data wire to its side wires or to all centre wires under one label. Suppressing $K$, with $t_X,t_Y$ the indicators of the $X$-bank and $Y$-bank triples concerned (for a side wire, the two neighbours it joins), the labels along each wire, times bracketed, are

$$
\begin{aligned}\text{centre: }&0\to B\otimes F\,(1)\to A\otimes F\,(3)\to B\otimes F\,(4)\to A\otimes F\,(6)\to\mathcal F\\ \text{side: }&0\to B\otimes\langle t_Y\rangle\,(0)\to(B\otimes F)\perp(P\otimes\langle t_X\rangle)\,(2)\to(B\otimes F)\perp(P\otimes t_Y^\perp)\,(5)\to A\otimes F\,(7)\to\mathcal F\\ X:\ &A\otimes\langle t_X\rangle\to(B\otimes F)\perp(P\otimes\langle t_X\rangle)\,(2)\to A\otimes F\,(3,6,7)\\ Y:\ &B\otimes\langle t_Y\rangle\,(0)\to B\otimes F\,(1,4)\to(B\otimes F)\perp(P\otimes t_Y^\perp)\,(5)\end{aligned}
$$

The same splitting shows every arrow is an inclusion except centre $3\to4$, which drops $P\otimes F$, of dimension $h$; side $2\to5$ is an inclusion because neighbours give $t_X\perp t_Y$, so $\langle t_X\rangle\subset t_Y^\perp$. With $I$ invocations of $c_{\rm b}$ or $c_{\rm c}$ centre wires each, $L_{\rm b}=Ic_{\rm b}h$ and $L_{\rm c}=Ic_{\rm c}h$. As $I=3\binom h3^2$ and $N=\binom h3^3$, $L_{\rm b}/N=3c_{\rm b}h/\binom h3$, so $L_{\rm b}/N=100/539$ and $L_{\rm c}/N=101/539$, both below $1/2$ <cite data-source="paper">\frac{L_{\rm b}}{N}=\frac{100}{539},\qquad \frac{L_{\rm c}}{N}=\frac{101}{539}</cite>.

In the bit network a vertex labelled $U$ gets the rational projection $\Pi_U$ onto $U$ along $U^\perp$, except that $X_a$'s source gets $-\Pi_{U_a}$ and other sources get $0$. An edge costs one smaller call per unit of rank of its matrix difference <cite data-source="paper">implements an edge change with one smaller interchange per unit of its rational rank</cite>. For $U\subset V$, $\Pi_V-\Pi_U$ is the identity on $V\cap U^\perp$ and zero on $U\perp V^\perp$, so its rank, like its negative's, is $\dim V-\dim U$. Only $X_a$'s first edge, into a gate labelled $U_a$, differs: $2\Pi_{U_a}$ has rank one where the dimension change is zero. So $s_{\rm b}=(W_{\rm b}m-2N+2L_{\rm b})+N=W_{\rm b}m-(N-2L_{\rm b})<W_{\rm b}m$ <cite data-source="paper">=W_{\rm b}m-N+2L_{\rm b} =177176569088785861287000000<W_{\rm b}m</cite>. The endpoint differences, $\Pi_{U_a^\perp}+\Pi_{U_a}$ from $X_a$ to its output $Y_a$ and $\mathrm{Id}-0$ on every other route, all equal $\mathrm{Id}$, so every route applies the same frame change. [Binary phase frames](phase-frames.md) give the complex network $s_{\rm c}=W_{\rm c}m-2N+2L_{\rm c}<W_{\rm c}m$.

The saving is positive because $L_{\rm b}/N<1/2$, but tiny: the relative deficit is $(W_{\rm b}m-s_{\rm b})/(W_{\rm b}m)=339/22587335000000\approx1.5\cdot10^{-11}$ <cite data-source="paper">=\frac{339}{22587335000000}</cite>, since $N-2L_{\rm b}<N$ while side wires make $W_{\rm b}m\approx4\cdot10^{10}N$.
