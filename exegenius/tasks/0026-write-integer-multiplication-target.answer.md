This page states the main theorem, the target of the argument: a deterministic multitape Turing machine multiplies two integers exactly in worst-case time beating the conjectured optimum.

Here $n$ is the number of bits in each of the two input integers. Here $O(g)$ means at most a constant times $g$ for all large inputs. The logarithm $\lg n = \max\{\lceil \log_2 n\rceil, 1\}$ is the base-2 logarithm rounded up and at least 1. The constant $\kappa = 2^{-182}$ is the power-saving exponent, the fixed amount by which the power of $\lg n$ falls below one. The theorem bounds the time by $O(n(\lg n)^{1-\kappa})$.

The argument has three steps: the product is reduced to a digit convolution, that convolution is computed through transform layers whose tape cost saves a power, and the exact product is recovered by rounding. Each transform level scans $\Theta(n)$ bits, so only savings in both movement and arithmetic beat $n\log n$ <cite data-source="paper">We therefore need savings in both movement and arithmetic.</cite> The result disproves the [Schönhage–Strassen n log n conjecture](nlogn-conjecture.md).

The product is reduced to the carried convolution of the digit sequences, as in [Multiplication as convolution of digits](convolution-reduction.md). The digit list is placed by the [Chinese-remainder address map](chinese-remainder-address-map.md) on a product of distinct prime cyclic axes. The multiplier computes its transforms by [Gaussian resampling](gaussian-resampling.md), which expresses a prime-length transform through power-of-two axes. The middle transform is converted by [Bluestein's chirp identity](bluestein-chirp.md) into a normalized cyclic convolution. The last cyclic coordinate is handled in the [polynomial ring modulo $y^r+1$](polynomial-ring-y.md), so that its wrap is a signed shift. The two forward transforms are carried out by the [synthetic polynomial transform](synthetic-transform.md), which keeps frequency order through the pointwise product. Each transform level is built from [simultaneous butterfly layers](simultaneous-butterfly-layer.md), each acting on one address bit. The address permutations are performed by [fast address interchange](address-interchange.md).

The power saving follows from [Recursive power saving](recursive-power-saving.md), so both tape procedures save a power of their parameter, with exponent strictly below one. Polynomial products are computed by [signed Kronecker packing](kronecker-packing.md) and the [Harvey–van der Hoeven O(n log n) multiplication](harvey-hoeven-multiplier.md). The exact product is recovered by [exact recovery by rounding](rounding-recovery.md): the coefficient error is below $1/2$, so rounding and carries give the binary product.
