## This page

Concept: The established O(N log N) multiplier
Gloss: The Harvey–van der Hoeven fixed-tape multiplier with cost O(N log 2N) on N-bit inputs, used only as an ordinary subroutine on short inputs.

## Where the argument relies on this page

- On disk-grid arrays over $\mathcal R_r=\mathbb C[y]/(y^r+1)$ indexed by a product of cyclic axes with $T$ coefficients in all and $M=T/r$ records, the normalized convolution $\mathcal M_r(f,g)=(f*g)/T$ is approximated with error below $\sqrt2 M(3\ell+1)2^{-p}$ in time $O(T p[d K+p K^{\tau-1}+\ell d^{\lambda'}+\log(r p)])$, where $\ell=\log_2 r$. (on [Normalized convolution over the ring](synthetic-convolution.md))
- Two disk-grid polynomials $f,g$ in $\mathcal R_r$ with $p$-bit components can be multiplied exactly and returned as $Q_p(fg/r)$ in time $O(r p\log(r p))$, by packing signed coefficients into big integers (Kronecker substitution) and calling an $O(N\log N)$ integer multiplier on four pairs. (on [Signed packing for ring products](kronecker-packing.md))
- For coprime $s_i<t_i$ with enough separation, the tensor Fourier transform $RF_{\boldsymbol s}$ together with its source permutation $R$ equals $2^\gamma\mathcal B_0QF_{\boldsymbol t}\mathcal A$ for contractions $\mathcal A,\mathcal B_0$ computed line by line on a padded box of $T$ entries in time $O(d T p^{3/2+\delta}\alpha)$ plus axis movements $O(T p\,d^2(1+\ell^\tau))$, with both coordinate permutations left inside the formula. (on [Resampling with permutations left in the transform](resampling-without-sorting.md))
- The procedure that extracts radix-$2^b$ digits, applies the Chinese-remainder address map $\Phi$, computes two forward source transforms, a pointwise product and the opposite transform with exact rescalings, rounds, and propagates carries outputs the exact $2n$-bit product. (on [The multiplication algorithm](multiplication-algorithm.md))

## Evidence from the sources, with the paragraph around each quote

- "$O(N\log(2N))$ on $N$-bit inputs" (paper)

  > To multiply the polynomial records, we use the fixed-tape integer multiplier of Harvey and van der Hoeven~\cite[Theorem~1.1]{HarveyHoeven2021}, with cost $O(N\log(2N))$ on $N$-bit inputs. This is an independent subroutine. Its inputs below have $O(rp)$ bits, and we include the conversions between coefficient streams and signed integers in the cost. The reduction is a signed form of Kronecker substitution; compare \cite[Lemma~2.5]{HarveyHoeven2021}. We specify the packing and recovery of coefficients to include negative values and the required tape format.

- "they do not invoke the improved theorem recursively" (paper)

  > More specifically, the proof adapts Harvey and van der Hoeven's Gaussian-resampling identity, contraction bounds, and short-convolution procedures~\cite[Theorem~4.2, Proposition~4.7 and Lemmas~4.8--4.12]{HarveyHoeven2021}. Their framework already permits arbitrary dimension. Here the source and target permutations remain in the transform identity, while the faster tape procedures perform the required axis movements. Bluestein's conversion to convolution~\cite{Bluestein1970} and the synthetic-root convolution algorithm are retained; the latter follows the polynomial-transform construction in~\cite[Section~2.4 and Section~3]{HarveyHoeven2021}. Signed coefficient packing is a form of Kronecker substitution, with the fixed-tape version of~\cite[Lemma~2.5]{HarveyHoeven2021} as a direct antecedent. Calls to the established $O(n\log n)$ multiplier are ordinary subroutine calls; they do not invoke the improved theorem recursively.
