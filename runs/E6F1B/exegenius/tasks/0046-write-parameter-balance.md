## This page in the argument

The step: With $p=\Theta(\log n)$, $d=\lfloor b^\epsilon\rfloor$ axes and ring dimension $r=2^{\Theta(p/d)}$, every row of the cost table has normalized power of $p$ at most $1-2\kappa$, so the total time is $O(n(\log n)^{1-\kappa})$.
How: fix all parameters and tiny exponents, then tabulate the normalized cost of each operation as a power of $p$ and compare with $1-2\kappa$
Why: The explicit minimum margin $2^{-181}=2\kappa$ leaves enough slack to absorb any remaining fixed power of $\log p$. (the sources say so: "This explicit slack will cover" (paper))

Its sub-steps, in order:

- The fixed rational exponents $\tau,\sigma,\lambda,\lambda',c,\epsilon,\delta$ and $\kappa=2^{-182}$ are chosen so that all layer, resampling and dimension conditions hold with room to spare.
- The seven dominant margins below one satisfy $\min_i g_i=2^{-181}=2\kappa$.
- Line-by-line Gaussian maps, CRT and axis layouts, chirps and twists, and packed polynomial products each cost a power of $p$ below the margin, with $p$ and $d$ as given.
- Descriptor setup, prime search, exceptional-address sorting and tape management across all recursive calls cost $o(Tp)$.
- Each of the seven displayed powers is at most $1-2\kappa$, so the complete large-input bound is $O(n(\log n)^{1-\kappa})$.

It serves these steps, from the main result down:

- There is one deterministic fixed-alphabet, fixed-tape Turing machine multiplying $n$-bit integers exactly in $O(n(\lg n)^{1-\kappa})$ time with $\kappa=2^{-182}$, disproving the $n\log n$ optimality conjecture in this model.; how: build two fast tape procedures from linear networks with a rank saving, insert them into a Gaussian-resampling and chirp reduction of the digit convolution to a ring convolution, and balance all parameters (on [Integer multiplication in O(n (lg n)^(1−κ)) time](main-theorem.md))

Source: "We next separate setup at recursion nodes from work repeated for every" (paper)
> \paragraph{Setup and exceptional streams across all calls.} We next separate setup at recursion nodes from work repeated for every polynomial record or every Gaussian line. This setup then costs at most $p^{A+C}=o(Tp)$.
