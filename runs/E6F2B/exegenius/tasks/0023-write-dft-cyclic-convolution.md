## This page

Concept: Normalized DFT and cyclic convolution
Gloss: The length-q discrete Fourier transform with 1/q normalization turns cyclic convolution into pointwise multiplication; its inverse uses the opposite sign of the exponent.

## Where the argument relies on this page

- For coprime lengths $s<t$ the coordinate permutations $P_s,P_t$ stay inside the identity $P_sF_s=2^{2\alpha^2}B_0P_tF_tA$, and tensoring it over $d$ axes gives $RF_{\boldsymbol s}=2^\gamma\mathcal B_0QF_{\boldsymbol t}\mathcal A$ with line-wise maps costing $O(dTp^{3/2+\delta}\alpha)$. (on [Resampling with the permutations left in the transform](resampling-no-sort.md))
- There are linear contractions $A:\mathbb C^s\to\mathbb C^t$ and $B_0:\mathbb C^t\to\mathbb C^s$ with $P_sF_s=2^{2\alpha^2}B_0P_tF_tA$.
- The chirp identity $QF_{\boldsymbol t}u=\overline a\cdot(a*(\overline a\cdot u))/T$ evaluates the target-permuted transform without applying $Q$, and the source permutation cancels: $(RF^+_{\boldsymbol s})[(RF_{\boldsymbol s}u)\cdot(RF_{\boldsymbol s}v)]=(u*v)/S^2$. (on [Chirp identity and source-permutation cancellation](chirp-permutation-cancel.md))
- Since $RF^+_{\boldsymbol s}R=F^+_{\boldsymbol s}$ and pointwise products commute with permutations, the source permutation $R$ drops out and only the normalization $1/S^2$ remains.
- The radix-digit coefficient list can be placed on a product of $d$ distinct prime cyclic axes by an isomorphism $\Phi$ that preserves cyclic convolution and is realized by triangular controlled shifts. (on [Chinese-remainder map to prime cyclic axes](crt-axis-map.md))
- Multiplying coefficient $k$ by $\zeta^k$ with $\zeta=e^{\pi i/r}$ is a ring isomorphism from $x^r=1$ to $y^r=-1$, and the negative wrap becomes the cyclic wrap after untwisting. (on [Twist from cyclic to negacyclic convolution](coefficient-twist.md))
- The two occurrences of $S$ undo different normalizations: the exact counterpart is $(u*v)/S^2$ after the three transforms, and $2^{2b+4}(u*v)$ after both scales.

## Pages that build on this one

- Multidimensional (tensor) Fourier transform: The tensor product of one-dimensional transforms along several axes; a transform on an array with axis lengths t_1,…,t_d is applied axis by axis.
- Chinese-remainder map to prime cyclic axes: An algebra isomorphism sending one cyclic convolution of length S = s_1⋯s_d (pairwise coprime) to a convolution on a product of d cyclic axes, implemented by triangular controlled cyclic shifts.
- Bluestein's chirp identity: Rewrites a Fourier transform as a pointwise phase multiplication, a cyclic convolution with a chirp array, and another pointwise phase multiplication.
- Twist from cyclic to negacyclic convolution: Multiplying coefficient k by ζ^k with ζ = e^{πi/r} is a ring isomorphism from the cyclic ring (x^r = 1) to the ring modulo y^r + 1, so its negative wrap yields the cyclic wrap after untwisting.
- Synthetic Fourier transform modulo y^r + 1: A Fourier transform over the ring modulo y^r + 1 whose roots of unity are powers of y, so twiddle multiplications are signed coefficient shifts; it satisfies a character-cancellation identity despite zero divisors.
