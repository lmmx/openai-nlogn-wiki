## This page

Concept: Rank deficit s < Wm
Gloss: The key property of both finite networks: the total number s of smaller calls needed over all edges is strictly less than W·m, so s/W < m.
Also called: rank saving, relative rank deficit
Heading: Rank deficit $s < Wm$

The symbols in this page's heading denote (say so on the page):

- $s$ (number of smaller calls): The total number of smaller recursive calls the network's edges require, summed over all edges.
- $W$ (wire count): The number of wires (roles) in the finite network, including scratch wires.
- $m$ (label dimension): The dimension of the label space, which is the factor by which each smaller call shrinks the recursion parameter.

## This page in the argument

The step: Two fixed finite networks (a bit network over $\mathbb F_2$ and a complex network over $\mathbb Z[i,1/2]$) exchange two banks while restoring scratch wires, and their label budgets lose $L<N/2$, so the total number $s$ of smaller calls their edges need satisfies $s<Wm$.
How: Construct the three-stage bank exchange on triples of a 100-element set, label it with nested nondegenerate subspaces, and count the label-dimension changes.
Why: The number of smaller operations an edge needs is its label-dimension change, so a strict deficit in the total change is exactly what yields the saving. (the sources say so: "The saving will come from keeping the total dimension lost on decreasing
edges small relative to $N$." (paper))
Striking feature: The networks must move every one of the $W$ roles, yet the total count $s$ of smaller calls stays strictly below $Wm$; this strict inequality is the only source of the power saving. (explain why it holds, from the method)

Its sub-steps, in order:

- A scalar linear network of $W$ wires, built from three stages of copy, gather, scatter and side-injection gates on triples, sends the two banks $(X_a,Y_a)$ to $(-Y_a,X_a)$ (a plain swap over $\mathbb F_2$) and returns every scratch wire to its arbitrary initial value. (on [Three-stage bank exchange](bank-exchange.md))
- Every gate and terminal of either network can be labelled by a nondegenerate subspace of the $m$-dimensional space $\mathcal F=F^{\otimes3}$ so that labels are nested along every edge and the only decreasing edges are the central-wire edges from time 3 to time 4, each losing dimension $h$; hence the absolute label changes sum to $Wm-2N+2L$ with $L=Ich$. (on [Nested subspace labels and orthogonal residuals](subspace-labels.md))
- The loss ratios are $L_{\rm b}/N=100/539$ and $L_{\rm c}/N=101/539$, both smaller than $1/2$.

It serves these steps, from the main result down:

- One deterministic fixed-alphabet, fixed-tape Turing machine computes the exact $2n$-bit product of two $n$-bit integers in $O(n(\lg n)^{1-\kappa})$ worst-case time with $\kappa=2^{-182}$, disproving the $n\log n$ conjecture in this model.; how: Reduce the product to radix-digit convolution, move it by a Chinese-remainder map, Gaussian resampling, chirps and a phase twist to convolution over $\mathbb C[y]/(y^r+1)$ computed by synthetic transforms; speed up the transforms' address movement and butterfly layers with two network-based tape procedures; then prove exact recovery and total the costs. (on [Integer multiplication in O(n (lg n)^{1−κ}) time](main-theorem.md))
- Two tape procedures save over plain scanning: address chunks of width $u$ are interchanged in $O(Vu^\tau)$ time, and a simultaneous normalized butterfly layer on $D\le d$ chunks costs $O(Vd^{\lambda'})$, with fixed $\tau,\lambda'<1$.; how: Build each procedure from its own fixed linear network that exchanges two banks and restores scratch wires, apply the network pointwise to row-split role streams with frames chosen so every edge is a few smaller calls, and solve the volume-normalized recurrence.

## Evidence from the sources, with the paragraph around each quote

- "The decisive property is that the total number $s$ of smaller calls satisfies $s<Wm$." (paper)

  > The finite construction in Section~\ref{sec:finite-networks} attaches subspaces of a fixed $m$-dimensional space to its gates and terminals. The subspaces at adjacent vertices are nested. Their dimension changes count the smaller operations needed on an edge, with an additional source contribution in the bit construction. The construction uses three-element subsets: gather and scatter create coefficients depending on intersection size, and auxiliary wires cancel the unwanted coefficients. Orthogonality of the corresponding indicator vectors permits the subspace assignments. The decisive property is that the total number $s$ of smaller calls satisfies $s<Wm$.

- "This strict inequality is the source of the power saving." (paper)

  > A recursive call at parameter $mf$ therefore uses $s$ calls at parameter $f$, each on a stream of volume $V/W$. The parameter counts field digits for address interchange and selected axes for a numerical layer. After dividing cost by volume, multiplying the parameter by $m$ increases the recursive contribution by $s/W<m$. This strict inequality is the source of the power saving. The later tape and arithmetic estimates preserve it, including the work of changing numerical address coordinates. Those changes are implemented by whole-chunk moves and controlled shifts, followed by a deterministic correction of the explicitly identified exceptional addresses.
