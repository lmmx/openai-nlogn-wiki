## This page

Concept: Cyclic convolution
Gloss: The product of two coefficient arrays indexed by ℤ/tℤ (or a product of such groups) as in multiplication of polynomials modulo x^t−1; the Fourier transform turns it into pointwise multiplication.

## Where the argument relies on this page

- On disk-grid arrays over $\mathcal R_r=\mathbb C[y]/(y^r+1)$ indexed by a product of cyclic axes with $T$ coefficients in all and $M=T/r$ records, the normalized convolution $\mathcal M_r(f,g)=(f*g)/T$ is approximated with error below $\sqrt2 M(3\ell+1)2^{-p}$ in time $O(T p[d K+p K^{\tau-1}+\ell d^{\lambda'}+\log(r p)])$, where $\ell=\log_2 r$. (on [Normalized convolution over the ring](synthetic-convolution.md))
- Over $\mathcal R_r=\mathbb C[y]/(y^r+1)$ with $\omega_t=y^{2r/t}$, the transform $F_t^-$ is computed by $\log_2 t$ levels of two-point butterflies $H_0$ and multiplications by powers of $y$, which are signed cyclic shifts of the $r$ coefficients; its output sits in bit-reversed order $B_tF_t^-$ and the reverse procedure computes $F_t^+B_t^{-1}$ from that order, so no reordering is needed. (on [Synthetic polynomial transform](synthetic-transform.md))
- Because $F_{\boldsymbol t}^-(f*g)=M(F_{\boldsymbol t}^-f)(F_{\boldsymbol t}^-g)$ and $F_{\boldsymbol t}^+F_{\boldsymbol t}^-=I/M$, the scaled opposite transform of the pointwise product equals $(f*g)/(rM)$, and the common record permutation $LB$ cancels.
- For even $t_i$, the permuted transform satisfies $QF_{\boldsymbol t}u=\overline a\cdot\frac{a*(\overline a\cdot u)}{T}$ with the chirp $a_{\boldsymbol j}=\exp(-\pi i\sum_i s_ij_i^2/t_i)$, and the source permutation cancels, $(RF^+_{\boldsymbol s})[(RF_{\boldsymbol s}u)\cdot(RF_{\boldsymbol s}v)]=(u*v)/S^2$ with $S=\prod_i s_i$. (on [Bluestein chirp identity](bluestein-chirp.md))
- With the normalized convolution rule $F_{\boldsymbol s}(u*v)=S(F_{\boldsymbol s}u)\cdot(F_{\boldsymbol s}v)$ and $F^+_{\boldsymbol s}F_{\boldsymbol s}=I/S$, the retained permutation cancels and the result is $(u*v)/S^2$.
- The procedure that extracts radix-$2^b$ digits, applies the Chinese-remainder address map $\Phi$, computes two forward source transforms, a pointwise product and the opposite transform with exact rescalings, rounds, and propagates carries outputs the exact $2n$-bit product. (on [The multiplication algorithm](multiplication-algorithm.md))
- With $P_i=\prod_{j<i}s_j$ and $\mu_i=P_i^{-1}\bmod s_i$, the map $\Phi:x\mapsto\prod_ix_i^{\mu_i}$ is an algebra isomorphism from $\mathbb C[x]/(x^S-1)$ to $\mathbb C[x_1,\ldots,x_d]/(x_i^{s_i}-1)$ that turns cyclic convolution of length $S$ into convolution on the product of cyclic axes, and it and its inverse are implemented by triangular controlled shifts in time $O(T p\,d^2(1+\ell^\tau))$. (on [Chinese-remainder address map](crt-address-map.md))
- The map $\Theta:x_d\mapsto\zeta y$ with $\zeta=e^{\pi i/r}$ is a norm-preserving algebra isomorphism from $\mathbb C[x_d]/(x_d^r-1)$ onto $\mathbb C[y]/(y^r+1)$, diagonal in the coefficient bases, so a cyclic wrap on the last axis becomes a negacyclic wrap and back. (on [Last-coordinate phase twist](phase-twist.md))

## Evidence from the sources, with the paragraph around each quote

- "let $*$ denote cyclic convolution in the displayed" (paper)

  > For arrays on $\prod_i\mathbb Z/t_i\mathbb Z$ with values in $\mathcal R_r$, let $*$ denote cyclic convolution in the displayed axes and define \[ \mathcal M_r(f,g)=\frac{f*g}{rM}=\frac{f*g}{T}. \] For a fixed output coefficient, each of the $M$ first cyclic addresses determines one second address, and each of the $r$ first polynomial indices determines one second index modulo $r$. The negacyclic wrap supplies the sign. Thus that coefficient averages $rM$ signed scalar products, and $\mathcal M_r$ is a bilinear contraction: \[ \|\mathcal M_r(f,g)\|_\infty \leq\|f\|_\infty\|g\|_\infty. \] If the other operand is a disk array, replacing one operand changes the output by at most the norm distance between its two values.
