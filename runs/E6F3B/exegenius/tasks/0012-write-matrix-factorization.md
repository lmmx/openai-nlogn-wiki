## This page

Concept: Lower-triangular factorization of a matrix
Gloss: Every rational m×m matrix A of rank a factors as E₁ Π E₂ with E₁, E₂ invertible lower triangular and Π a partial permutation with exactly a ones.

## This page in the argument

The step: Every rational $m\times m$ matrix $A$ of rank $a$ factors as $E_1\Pi E_2$ with $E_1,E_2$ invertible lower triangular and $\Pi$ a partial permutation matrix with exactly $a$ ones.

It serves these steps, from the main result down:

- A single deterministic multitape Turing machine multiplies $n$-bit integers exactly in $O(n(\lg n)^{1-\kappa})$ time with $\kappa=2^{-182}$, refuting the $n\log n$ optimality conjecture in this model.; how: Build two fast tape procedures, a bit-chunk interchange and a simultaneous butterfly layer, use them to evaluate the transforms of a digit convolution on an array of ring records, assemble the exact multiplier, and total its cost. (on [Sub-n-log-n integer multiplication](main-theorem.md))
- Two disjoint equal-width bit chunks of $u$ bits in an address array can be swapped in $O(Vu^\tau)$ steps for a fixed $\tau<1$, by XOR combinations of data whose final effect is an exact permutation.; how: Reduce arbitrary chunk widths to power-width interchanges, which are obtained from a fixed linear network with a strict rank saving. (on [Address chunk interchange](chunk-interchange.md))
- Two disjoint address chunks of $e$ radix-$q$ digits each, with $e$ a power of $m$ and enough rows, can be interchanged in $O(Ve^\tau)$ time.; how: Apply the fixed bit network to $W$ cyclic role streams, implementing every edge change by smaller shears and hence smaller interchanges, one per unit of edge rank, and solve the resulting recurrence. (on [Power-width chunk interchange](power-width-interchange.md))
- For a rational $m\times m$ matrix $A$ of rank $a$, the shear $(H,D)\mapsto(H+AD,D)$ over $\mathbb Z/q^b\mathbb Z$ takes exactly $a$ interchanges of $b$-digit fields plus $O(V)$ further work.; how: Factor $A$ into lower-triangular pieces, apply them with controlled shifts, and turn each one of the remaining partial permutation into a single field interchange. (on [Cost of a matrix shear on address fields](matrix-shear.md))
