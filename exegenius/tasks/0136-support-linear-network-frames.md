# exegenius task 0136-support-linear-network-frames

Project directory: `/mnt/openai-nlogn` (paths below are relative to it)

Check whether each quotation on a page of an explanatory wiki supports the claim it is attached to. Writers back claims taken from the sources with a quotation copied from them; a quotation can exist in the source and still not support what it is attached to.

- Judge each claim against its quotation alone, not against what you know or what the source may say elsewhere.
- `supported`: the quotation states the claim or directly implies it.
- `partial`: the quotation supports part of the claim, or a weaker version of it.
- `unsupported`: the quotation does not address what the claim asserts.
- `contradicted`: the quotation says something incompatible with the claim.
- For every label but `supported`, say in one line what is missing or wrong.
- Label every quotation listed, each once.

## Quotations

### 1

Claim: The network is fixed in two senses: its wiring does not depend on the data, and each wire has a fixed place in it, which the paper calls its role

Quotation, from Integer multiplication below n log n (OpenAI, 2026): "A wire’s fixed place in the network is called its role."

Paragraph:

> The network is fixed in two senses: its wiring does not depend on the data, and each wire has a fixed place in it, which the paper calls its role. Each procedure in the paper uses its own fixed linear network, which exchanges two banks of values and restores every auxiliary value. The auxiliary wires are scratch space: they may hold arbitrary values during the computation, but they end with the values they started with.
