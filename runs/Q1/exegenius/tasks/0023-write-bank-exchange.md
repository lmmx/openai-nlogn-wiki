## This page

Concept: Three-stage bank exchange
Gloss: The scalar network that, using copy, gather, scatter and side-injection gates per coordinate stage, maps two banks (X, Y) to (−Y, X) (a pure swap over 𝔽₂) while restoring all scratch wires.
Also called: gather, scatter, side injection, forward invocation

## This page in the argument

The step: A scalar linear network of $W$ wires, built from three stages of copy, gather, scatter and side-injection gates on triples, sends the two banks $(X_a,Y_a)$ to $(-Y_a,X_a)$ (a plain swap over $\mathbb F_2$) and returns every scratch wire to its arbitrary initial value.
How: Run an eight-row gate schedule per invocation that first subtracts the effect of the old scratch values, then injects $x$ into $y$ and finally undoes its own auxiliary updates; compose a forward, an inverse and a forward invocation over the three triple coordinates.
Why: Subtracting the old scratch contribution before using the scratch wires makes the net effect independent of their arbitrary initial values, and three add/subtract stages $y\gets y+x$, $x\gets x-y$, $y\gets y+x$ realize an exchange of banks. (the sources say so: "The first two rows of
the following schedule remove the effect of the old scratch values;
the next four introduce the source values and add their effect to the
target; the last two restore the scratch values." (paper))

Its sub-steps, in order:

- For arbitrary initial scratch values a forward invocation adds $x_S$ to $y_S$ and changes nothing else, because the central coefficient ($|S\cap T|\bmod 2$, or $(|S\cap T|-1)/2$) and the side coefficient cancel for all neighbouring distinct triples.
- Forward, inverse and forward invocations at stages 1, 2, 3 take $(x,y)$ to $(x,y+x)$, then $(-y,y+x)$, then $(-y,x)$.

It serves these steps, from the main result down:

- One deterministic fixed-alphabet, fixed-tape Turing machine computes the exact $2n$-bit product of two $n$-bit integers in $O(n(\lg n)^{1-\kappa})$ worst-case time with $\kappa=2^{-182}$, disproving the $n\log n$ conjecture in this model.; how: Reduce the product to radix-digit convolution, move it by a Chinese-remainder map, Gaussian resampling, chirps and a phase twist to convolution over $\mathbb C[y]/(y^r+1)$ computed by synthetic transforms; speed up the transforms' address movement and butterfly layers with two network-based tape procedures; then prove exact recovery and total the costs. (on [Integer multiplication in O(n (lg n)^{1−κ}) time](main-theorem.md))
- Two tape procedures save over plain scanning: address chunks of width $u$ are interchanged in $O(Vu^\tau)$ time, and a simultaneous normalized butterfly layer on $D\le d$ chunks costs $O(Vd^{\lambda'})$, with fixed $\tau,\lambda'<1$.; how: Build each procedure from its own fixed linear network that exchanges two banks and restores scratch wires, apply the network pointwise to row-split role streams with frames chosen so every edge is a few smaller calls, and solve the volume-normalized recurrence.
- Two fixed finite networks (a bit network over $\mathbb F_2$ and a complex network over $\mathbb Z[i,1/2]$) exchange two banks while restoring scratch wires, and their label budgets lose $L<N/2$, so the total number $s$ of smaller calls their edges need satisfies $s<Wm$.; how: Construct the three-stage bank exchange on triples of a 100-element set, label it with nested nondegenerate subspaces, and count the label-dimension changes. (on [Rank deficit s < Wm](rank-deficit.md))

> \begin{lemma}\label{lem:scalar-motifs} For arbitrary initial values of its auxiliary wires, a forward invocation changes $y_S$ to $y_S+x_S$ and leaves every other value unchanged.
