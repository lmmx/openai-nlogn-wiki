A last-coordinate phase twist is a coefficientwise rescaling that makes the last cyclic axis of a convolution behave like a product of polynomials, so that the remaining axes can be convolved over a polynomial ring. It is applied after [Bluestein's chirp identity](bluestein-chirp.md) has turned the middle transform into a cyclic convolution.

Let $k$ and $l$ be exponents on the last axis, with $0\le k,l<r$. Let $z$ be the variable of the cyclic ring $\mathbb C[z]/(z^r-1)$, whose exponents wrap modulo $r$. Let $\zeta=e^{\pi \mathrm i/r}$, where $\mathrm i$ is the imaginary unit, so $\zeta^r=-1$. Let $y$ be the variable of $\mathbb C[y]/(y^r+1)$, where $y^r=-1$. The twist is the ring map $z\mapsto \zeta y$, well defined since $(\zeta y)^r=1$. The twist turns the last cyclic coordinate into the [Polynomial ring modulo $y^r+1$](poly-ring-y-r.md). Applied to $\sum_k c_k z^k$ it gives $\sum_k c_k\zeta^k y^k$, so it multiplies coefficient $k$ by $\zeta^k$, and untwisting multiplies it by $\zeta^{-k}$.

Every sum $k+l$ can be written $k+l=j+hr$ with $0\le j<r$ and $h\in\{0,1\}$, where $j$ is the cyclic index of the product and $h$ records whether the exponent wrapped. The cyclic product is $z^{k+l}=z^j$. In $y$-powers, $y^{k+l}=(-1)^h y^j$, because $y^r=-1$. The twist contributes $\zeta^{k+l}=(-1)^h\zeta^j$, because $\zeta^r=-1$. The two signs cancel, so $(\zeta^k y^k)(\zeta^l y^l)=\zeta^j y^j$. Untwisting this term by $\zeta^{-j}$ leaves exactly $z^j$, the original cyclic term. The negative wrap is therefore the original cyclic wrap once the twist is removed <cite data-source="paper">its negative wrap gives precisely the original cyclic wrap after the twist is removed</cite>.

For example, if $r=2$ then $\zeta=\mathrm i$, and $(\mathrm iy)(\mathrm iy)=-y^2=1$ modulo $y^2+1$, which untwists to $z\cdot z=1$.

For the other axes, let $v$ be an index tuple with one coordinate per remaining cyclic axis, each wrapping modulo its length. Let $R=\mathbb C[y]/(y^r+1)$, so each entry on those axes is an element of $R$. Write $\alpha_k$ and $\beta_l$ for the untwisted coefficients of two entries, so the entries are $\sum_k \alpha_k\zeta^k y^k$ and $\sum_l \beta_l\zeta^l y^l$. By the same sign cancellation, their product in $R$ has $y^j$-coefficient $\zeta^j\sum_{k+l\equiv j \ (\mathrm{mod}\ r)}\alpha_k\beta_l$, so untwisting by $\zeta^{-j}$ gives their cyclic product. The twist leaves the other axes unchanged, so the product on those axes is a convolution over $R$. The twist rescales the coefficients of a [Convolution](convolution.md) so that its cyclic wrap on the last axis becomes a negative wrap in $R$.
