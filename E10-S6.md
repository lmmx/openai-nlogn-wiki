- `runs/S6F2SM`, `S6F4SM` (Sonnet `medium`) and `S6F2HH`, `S6F4HH` (Haiku `high`): the same instruction, on F2 and F4.
- Every task file was checked by diff against its comparison's, apart from the path. Each prompt went word for word to a fresh `general-purpose` subagent with the stated model and effort.
- `results/transcripts/e10-s6/subagents/`: the eight subagents' transcripts and `.meta.json`.
