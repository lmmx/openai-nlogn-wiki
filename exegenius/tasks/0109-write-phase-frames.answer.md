Binary phase frames attach a basis to each label of [Nested subspace labels and rank deficit](subspace-labels.md), so each edge is a product of two-point kernels, one per dimension of change, as the recursion needs.

Labels are subspaces of $\mathbb F_2^m$, with $m$ the label-space dimension; $u,v,w,z$ are vectors of $\mathbb F_2^m$ here. Write $\operatorname{wt}(x)$ for Hamming weight, $[b]\in\{0,1\}$ for the integer representative of $b\in\mathbb F_2$, $P_U$ for projection onto a label $U$, and $z\cdot w$ for the dot product, both over $\mathbb F_2$, so $z\cdot z\equiv\operatorname{wt}(z)\pmod 2$. Put $q_U(x)=\operatorname{wt}(P_Ux)\bmod 4$. With $H=\tfrac1{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}$, the phase frame is $\mathcal C_U=H^{\otimes m}\operatorname{diag}_x(i^{q_U(x)})H^{\otimes m}$ on arrays indexed by $x\in\mathbb F_2^m$, with Gaussian-dyadic entries. <cite data-source="paper">Each entry of $\mathcal C_U$ and its inverse is a Gaussian integer divided by $2^m$</cite> Let $C=H\operatorname{diag}(1,i)H=aI+bX$ with $a=\frac{1+i}{2}$, $b=\frac{1-i}{2}$, and $X_v$ the translation $(X_vf)(x)=f(x+v)$.

For binary vectors $z,w$, $\operatorname{wt}(z+w)=\operatorname{wt}(z)+\operatorname{wt}(w)-2|\operatorname{supp}(z)\cap\operatorname{supp}(w)|$. If $z\cdot w=0$ the intersection cardinality is even, so weight is additive modulo four on orthogonal vectors.

Take an edge from $U$ to $V=U\perp E$, where the residual $E$ has an orthonormal basis $\mathcal B$. Then $P_Vx=P_Ux+\sum_{v\in\mathcal B}[v\cdot x]\,v$ (orthogonal summands), so $q_V(x)-q_U(x)=\sum_{v\in\mathcal B}\operatorname{wt}(v)[v\cdot x]\pmod 4$, negated on a decreasing edge, and each $\operatorname{wt}(v)$ is odd since $v\cdot v=1$. Each term is thus $\varepsilon[v\cdot x]$ with $\varepsilon=\pm1$, including the edge direction. The function $i^{\varepsilon[v\cdot x]}$ equals $\frac{1+i^\varepsilon}2+\frac{1-i^\varepsilon}2(-1)^{v\cdot x}$.

Since $H^2=I$, $\mathcal C_V\mathcal C_U^{-1}=H^{\otimes m}\operatorname{diag}(i^{q_V-q_U})H^{\otimes m}$. The character $(-1)^{v\cdot x}$ is $\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$ on the coordinates in $\operatorname{supp}(v)$. Since $H\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)H=X$, the character $(-1)^{v\cdot x}$ becomes the translation $X_v$, so the phase becomes $aI+bX_v$ or its inverse ($\varepsilon=-1$ swaps $a,b$; $a^2+b^2=0$, $2ab=1$). Each edge factor is a translate $aI+bX_v$ of [Two-point kernel C](phase-kernel.md), a butterfly-type step, so a residual of dimension $d$ costs $d$ factors.

Let $L_{\rm c}$ be the total dimension lost on decreasing edges of the complex network, with $W_{\rm c}$ wires. Edge dimension changes sum to net gain plus twice the losses; data wires gain $m-1$, scratch wires $m$, net $W_{\rm c}m-2N$. So the number $s_{\rm c}$ of translation-kernel factors is $W_{\rm c}m-2N+2L_{\rm c}=1873807244636671267308000000<W_{\rm c}m$ <cite data-source="paper">s_{\rm c}=W_{\rm c}m-2N+2L_{\rm c}</cite>, because $L_{\rm c}/N=101/539<1/2$. The deficit of $s_{\rm c}$ from $W_{\rm c}m$ is $2N-2L_{\rm c}>0$.

Roles (wire names) are scratch wires and data banks $X_a,Y_a$, indexed by $a$ (not the coefficient). Source to sink labels are $U_a\to\mathcal F$ on $X_a$, $0\to U_a^\perp$ on $Y_a$, and $0\to\mathcal F$ on scratch, with $U_a$ the line spanned by $u_a=t_{a_1}\otimes t_{a_2}\otimes t_{a_3}$, each $t_{a_j}$ the indicator of a triple. For $k$ columns (addresses are $k$-tuples), [Frames on wire arrays](array-frames.md) gives each role the $k$th tensor power of sink frame times inverse source frame, with $X_a,Y_a$ exchanged.

Label $0$ has frame $I$. For $\mathcal F$, $q_{\mathcal F}(x)=\operatorname{wt}(x)$ and $i^{\operatorname{wt}(x)}=\prod_ji^{x_j}$, so its frame is $C^{\otimes m}$. So scratch roles and $Y_a\to X_a$ get $C^{\otimes m}$; only $X_a\to Y_a$, from $U_a$ to $U_a^\perp$, differs.

Take $u=u_a$, with $3^3=27$ ones, so $u\cdot u=1$ and $P_{U_a}x=[u\cdot x]u$. The orthogonal parts of $x=P_{U_a}x+P_{U_a^\perp}x$ give $\operatorname{wt}(x)\equiv q_{U_a}+q_{U_a^\perp}$, hence $q_{U_a^\perp}-q_{U_a}=\operatorname{wt}(x)-2\operatorname{wt}(u)[u\cdot x]=\operatorname{wt}(x)+2[u\cdot x]\pmod 4$. As $2[u\cdot x]\equiv2\sum_{j\in\operatorname{supp}(u)}x_j$, the phase per coordinate is $i^{-x_j}$ on the 27 coordinates of $\operatorname{supp}(u)$ and $i^{x_j}$ elsewhere, so the operator is $C^{-1}$ there and $C$ elsewhere.

With $Z=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$, $ZXZ=-X$, $C^{-1}=bI+aX$, $ib=a$ and $ia=-b$ give $C=iZC^{-1}Z$. Let $Z_{a,k}$ be $Z$ on those 27 coordinates in every column. Apply $Z_{a,k}$ before the network at $X_a$ and $i^{27k}Z_{a,k}$ after it at $Y_a$: each of the $27k$ inverse factors becomes $C$. Negating the physical $X$ outputs, which carry the scalar exchange's minus sign, and renaming outputs to input roles leaves every role, scratch included, with $C^{\otimes mk}$.
