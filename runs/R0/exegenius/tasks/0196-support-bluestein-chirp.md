- Judge each claim against its quotation alone, not against what you know or what the source may say elsewhere.

- For every label but `supported`, say in one line what is missing or wrong.
- Label every quotation listed, each once.

## Quotations

### 1

Claim: For odd $n$, $z_{e+n}=-z_e$, and the phase twist below turns this wrap into the original cyclic wrap

Quotation, from Integer multiplication below n log n (OpenAI, 2026): "after the twist is removed"

Paragraph:

> Reading $z$ at $j-k$ modulo $n$ makes the sum cyclic, but the wrap carries a sign. Since $\zeta^n$ squares to $\omega^n=1$ and is not $1$, $\zeta^n=-1$. Expanding $(e+n)^2=e^2+2en+n^2$ gives $z_{e+n}=(-1)^n z_e$, using $\omega^{en}=1$. For odd $n$, $z_{e+n}=-z_e$, and the phase twist below turns this wrap into the original cyclic wrap.
