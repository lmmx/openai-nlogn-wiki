## Where the argument relies on this page

- A fixed network of copy, gather, scatter and side-injection gates on banks $X$ and $Y$ sends $(X_a,Y_a)$ to $(-Y_a,X_a)$ in three stages and restores every scratch wire to its arbitrary initial value. (on [Linear network exchanging two banks with restored scratch](scalar-exchange-network.md))
- If each wire's scalar is replaced by an array and every gate and terminal carries an invertible frame operator, the network acts as $D_{\rm out}\,\mathsf S_\Omega\,D_{\rm in}^{-1}$, so only the endpoint frames matter and each edge from $u$ to $v$ is implemented by $D_vD_u^{-1}$. (on [Frames and the common-frame identity](network-frames.md))
- Gates and terminals can be labelled by nested nondegenerate subspaces of the $m$-dimensional space $\mathcal F=F^{\otimes 3}$, built from tensor powers of triple indicator vectors, so that the total dimension change over the edges of a network with $W$ wires is below $W\cdot m$. (on [Nested subspace labels and the rank deficit](subspace-labels-rank-saving.md))
- Every rational $m\times m$ matrix $A$ of rank $a$ factors as $A=E_1\Pi E_2$ with $E_1,E_2$ invertible lower triangular and $\Pi$ a partial permutation matrix with exactly $a$ ones. (on [Lower-triangular factorization of a matrix](lower-factorization.md))

## Pages that build on this one

- Tensor products of vectors, spaces and operators: The tensor (Kronecker) product of vectors, spaces and operators, which acts factor by factor on coordinates of a multi-index.
- Address shear: The invertible permutation of array entries sending address (H,D) to (H+MD,D) for a matrix M, which composes additively and, combined with two linear-cost updates, interchanges the H and D fields.
- Binary vectors, XOR and Hamming weight: Vectors over the two-element field with addition equal to XOR, dot product mod 2, and Hamming weight wt(x), the number of ones.
- Nondegenerate bilinear forms and orthogonal complements: A symmetric bilinear form defines orthogonality; for a nondegenerate subspace the space splits as the subspace plus its orthogonal complement, and over F₂ a nonalternating form has an orthonormal basis.
- Linear network exchanging two banks with restored scratch: A fixed network of linear gates (copy, gather, scatter, side injection) on banks X and Y that sends (X,Y) to (−Y,X) in three stages while restoring every auxiliary scratch wire to its arbitrary initial value.
- Frames and the common-frame identity: Replace each wire's scalar by an array and give each gate/terminal an invertible frame operator; since a common frame commutes with pointwise linear gates, the whole network acts as D_out S D_in⁻¹, so only endpoint frames matter and each edge can be implemented by D_v D_u⁻¹.
- Lower-triangular factorization of a matrix: Every rational matrix A of rank a factors as E₁ Π E₂ with E₁,E₂ invertible lower triangular and Π a partial permutation with exactly a ones (a Bruhat-type factorization in reversed coordinate order).
- Phase kernel C = aI + bX: The two-point Gaussian dyadic kernel C = H S H = aI + bX with a=(1+i)/2, b=(1−i)/2, satisfying C²=X, C⁴=I, C⁻¹ = −iZCZ and H₀ = b S C S, which relates a normalized butterfly to C.
