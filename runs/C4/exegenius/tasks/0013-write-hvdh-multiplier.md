## This page

Concept: Harvey–van der Hoeven O(n log n) multiplier as subroutine
Gloss: The established O(N log N) fixed-tape integer multiplier, used as an ordinary subroutine for short polynomial records.

## This page in the argument

The step: Two polynomials in the ring modulo $y^r+1$ with disk coefficients are multiplied exactly in $O(rp\log(rp))$ time.
How: Pack signed coefficients into one integer (Kronecker substitution) and call the established integer multiplier as an ordinary subroutine.
Why: A polynomial product is an integer product after packing, so the known $O(N\log N)$ multiplier handles it; being short records of $O(rp)$ bits, its cost is acceptable. (the sources say so: "To multiply the polynomial records, we use the fixed-tape integer" (paper))

Its sub-steps, in order:

- Signed coefficients including negative ones are packed into one integer so that the polynomial product becomes an integer product. (on [Signed Kronecker packing](kronecker-packing.md))
- Both inputs are packed, multiplied by the Harvey--van der Hoeven multiplier, and unpacked to give the exact product and the rounded $fg/r$.

It serves these steps, from the main result down:

- Two $n$-bit integers are multiplied exactly by a deterministic fixed-tape machine in time $O(n(\lg n)^{1-\kappa})$ with $\kappa=2^{-182}$.; how: Reduce the product to convolution through a chain of exact changes of representation, supply the movement and butterfly cost savings by two fast tape procedures, and assemble parameters, precision and cost. (on [Integer multiplication in O(n (lg n)^(1−κ)) time](main-theorem.md))
- Convolution over $\mathcal R_r=\mathbb C[y]/(y^r+1)$ with $r$ coefficients per record is computed with synthetic transforms laid out on tape, a pointwise product and the opposite transform, with the stored frequency order retained.; how: Use polynomial roots of unity $y^{2r/t}$ so the transforms are signed shifts and butterflies, arrange tape layout so the parallel layers apply, then multiply pointwise by exact ring products. (on [Synthetic transform layout and convolution](transform-layout.md))
