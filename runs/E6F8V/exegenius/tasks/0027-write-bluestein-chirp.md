## This page

Concept: Bluestein chirp identity
Gloss: Rewriting a Fourier transform, including a frequency permutation, as pointwise chirp phases around a cyclic convolution with the chirp.

## This page in the argument

The step: The middle transform with its frequency permutation is $QF_{\boldsymbol t}u=\overline a\cdot\bigl(a*(\overline a\cdot u)\bigr)/T$ for the chirp $a_{\boldsymbol j}=\exp(-\pi i\sum_i s_ij_i^2/t_i)$: chirp phases around one cyclic convolution.
How: Expand the product of the chirp phases $\overline a_{\boldsymbol j}a_{\boldsymbol j-\boldsymbol k}\overline a_{\boldsymbol k}$ so that the squares cancel and only the cross term $2\pi i\sum s_ij_ik_i/t_i$ remains.
Why: The chirp identity performs the frequency change in $Q$ without applying $Q$ as a separate permutation of the array. (the sources say so: "multiplications. The chirp identity below performs the frequency change" (paper))

Its sub-steps, in order:

- For the chirp $a$, $QF_{\boldsymbol t}u=\overline a\cdot(a*(\overline a\cdot u))/T$.
- The chirp $a$ has period $t_i$ in coordinate $i$, so it is a well defined cyclic kernel.
- The coefficient of $u_{\boldsymbol k}$ in the right side is $\frac1T\exp(2\pi i\sum_i s_ij_ik_i/t_i)$, exactly the coefficient in $QF_{\boldsymbol t}$.

It serves these steps, from the main result down:

- A deterministic multitape Turing machine multiplies two $n$-bit integers in $O(n(\lg n)^{1-\kappa})$ time with $\kappa=2^{-182}$.; how: Build two faster tape procedures (address chunk interchange and simultaneous butterfly layers) from rank-saving linear networks, use them in synthetic polynomial transforms and resampled source transforms for a convolution, reduce integer multiplication to that convolution, then prove exactness and add up costs. (on [Integer multiplication below n log n](main-theorem.md))
- A source Fourier transform on pairwise coprime (prime) lengths can be computed through power-of-two transforms, with the middle transform as a chirp convolution and with the coordinate permutations cancelling rather than being executed.; how: Use Gaussian resampling to pass from prime lengths to power-of-two lengths, Bluestein's chirp to turn the middle transform into a cyclic convolution, and an algebraic cancellation of the retained permutations.
