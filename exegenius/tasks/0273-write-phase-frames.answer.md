Binary phase frames are operators attached to the labels of [Nested subspace labels and rank deficit](subspace-labels.md) in the complex network, chosen so that every edge difference splits into one two-point kernel per dimension of change, which makes each residual dimension one smaller call of a butterfly-type operation, as the recursion needs.

Several letters change meaning on this page: $v$ is a binary vector (not a number of triples), $E$ is an edge residual (not a tensor factor), $k$ counts columns of data (not a depth), and $u$ is a binary vector (not an integer). Labels are subspaces of $\mathbb F_2^m$ ($m$ the label dimension), and $x,u,v,w,z$ are vectors of $\mathbb F_2^m$. The support $\operatorname{supp}(z)$ of a vector is the set of coordinates where it is $1$. Write $\operatorname{wt}(x)=|\operatorname{supp}(x)|$ for the Hamming weight, $[b]\in\{0,1\}$ for the integer representative of $b\in\mathbb F_2$, $P_U$ for the projection onto a label $U$, and $z\cdot w$ for the dot product, both over $\mathbb F_2$. A vector has binary norm one when $v\cdot v=1$, equivalently its weight is odd; a basis is orthonormal when its vectors have norm one and are pairwise orthogonal. Let $X_v$ be the translation $(X_vf)(x)=f(x+v)$, and let $C=H\operatorname{diag}(1,i)H=aI+bX$, with $a=\frac{1+i}{2}$, $b=\frac{1-i}{2}$.

For a label $U$ put $q_U(x)=\operatorname{wt}(P_Ux)\bmod 4$, with the projection taken over $\mathbb F_2$. The phase frame is $\mathcal C_U=H^{\otimes m}\operatorname{diag}_x(i^{q_U(x)})H^{\otimes m}$, with Gaussian-dyadic entries, since each entry of $\mathcal C_U$ and its inverse is a Gaussian integer divided by $2^m$ <cite data-source="paper">Each entry of $\mathcal C_U$ and its inverse is a Gaussian integer divided by $2^m$</cite>.

## Weight modulo four

Weight modulo four is the phase because it is additive on orthogonal vectors. For binary vectors $z,w$, $\operatorname{wt}(z+w)=\operatorname{wt}(z)+\operatorname{wt}(w)-2|\operatorname{supp}(z)\cap\operatorname{supp}(w)|$, since a coordinate in both supports holds $1+1=0$. If $z\cdot w=0$ the intersection cardinality is even, so weight is additive modulo four on orthogonal vectors.

Take an edge from $U$ to $V=U\perp E$, where $E$, the edge residual, has orthonormal basis $\mathcal B$. Then $P_Vx=P_Ux+\sum_{v\in\mathcal B}[v\cdot x]\,v$, a sum of pairwise orthogonal vectors. Hence for $V=U\perp E$ with orthonormal basis $\mathcal B$ of $E$: $q_V(x)-q_U(x)=\sum_{v\in\mathcal B}\operatorname{wt}(v)[v\cdot x]\pmod 4$ (negated on a decreasing edge), and each $\operatorname{wt}(v)$ is odd. Each term is therefore $\varepsilon[v\cdot x]$, where $\varepsilon=\pm1$ modulo four includes the sign of the edge direction. The function $i^{\varepsilon[v\cdot x]}$ with $\varepsilon=\pm1$ equals $\frac{1+i^\varepsilon}2+\frac{1-i^\varepsilon}2(-1)^{v\cdot x}$, as checking $v\cdot x=0,1$ shows.

## From phases to kernels

Since $H^2=I$, $\mathcal C_V\mathcal C_U^{-1}=H^{\otimes m}\operatorname{diag}(i^{q_V-q_U})H^{\otimes m}$, a product over $\mathcal B$. The character $(-1)^{v\cdot x}$ is $Z=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$ on each coordinate in $\operatorname{supp}(v)$. Since $H\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)H=X$, the character $(-1)^{v\cdot x}$ becomes the translation $X_v$, so the phase becomes $aI+bX_v$ or its inverse. For $\varepsilon=-1$ the coefficients swap, giving $bI+aX_v$, the inverse since $a^2+b^2=0$ and $2ab=1$. Each edge factor is a translate $aI+bX_v$ of [Two-point kernel C](phase-kernel.md), one per basis vector. Each factor combines only the entries $x,x+v$, so it is one butterfly-type pass, independent of the others.

Let $L_{\rm c}$ be the total dimension lost on decreasing edges, $N$ the bank size and $W_{\rm c}$ the wire count. The number of translation-kernel factors over the network is $s_{\rm c}=W_{\rm c}m-2N+2L_{\rm c}=1873807244636671267308000000<W_{\rm c}m$ <cite data-source="paper">s_{\rm c}=W_{\rm c}m-2N+2L_{\rm c}</cite>. The deficit of $s_{\rm c}$ from $W_{\rm c}m$ is $2N-2L_{\rm c}>0$, since $L_{\rm c}/N<\frac12$.

## Terminal pairs

Source and sink labels are $U_a\to\mathcal F$ on $X_a$, $0\to U_a^\perp$ on $Y_a$ and $0\to\mathcal F$ on scratch, with $U_a$ the line spanned by $u_a=t_{a_1}\otimes t_{a_2}\otimes t_{a_3}$. By [Frames on wire arrays](array-frames.md), each wire gets the sink frame of its output role times the inverse source frame of its input role, tensored over the $k$ columns. Label $0$ has frame $I$. For $\mathcal F$, $q_{\mathcal F}(x)=\operatorname{wt}(x)$ and $i^{\operatorname{wt}(x)}=\prod_ji^{x_j}$, so its frame is $C^{\otimes m}$. Only the pair $X_a\to Y_a$ differs.

For it take $u=u_a$, with $3^3=27$ ones, so $u\cdot u=1$ and $P_{U_a}x=[u\cdot x]u$. Since $P_{U_a}x$ and $P_{U_a^\perp}x$ are orthogonal and sum to $x$, $q_{U_a}+q_{U_a^\perp}\equiv\operatorname{wt}(x)$, so $q_{U_a^\perp}(x)-q_{U_a}(x)=\operatorname{wt}(x)-2\operatorname{wt}(u)[u\cdot x]=\operatorname{wt}(x)+2[u\cdot x]\pmod 4$. As $2[u\cdot x]\equiv2\sum_{j\in\operatorname{supp}(u)}x_j$, the phase per coordinate is $i^{-x_j}$ on the 27 coordinates of $\operatorname{supp}(u)$ and $i^{x_j}$ elsewhere, so the operator is $C^{-1}$ on the 27 coordinates of $\operatorname{supp}(u)$ and $C$ elsewhere.

With $ZXZ=-X$ and $C^{-1}=bI+aX$, and since $ib=a$ and $ia=-b$, we get $C=iZC^{-1}Z$, so $ZC^{-1}Z=-iC$. Let $Z_{a,k}$ be $Z$ on those 27 coordinates in every column. Apply $Z_{a,k}$ before the network at $X_a$ and $i^{27k}Z_{a,k}$ after it at $Y_a$. The identity $C=iZC^{-1}Z$ turns each of the $27k$ inverse factors into $C$ after applying $Z_{a,k}$ before and $i^{27k}Z_{a,k}$ after, because the $27k$ factors $-i$ cancel against $i^{27k}$. Once the physical $X$ outputs, which carry the minus sign of the scalar exchange, are negated and outputs are renamed to input roles, every role, including scratch roles, receives $C^{\otimes mk}$.
