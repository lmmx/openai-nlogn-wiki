## This page

Concept: Power-width interchange
Gloss: Interchanging two address chunks of e = mᵏ radix-q digits in O(V·e^τ) time by running the bit network on row-split role streams with shear frames and recursive smaller interchanges.

## This page in the argument

The step: For $e=m^k$, two disjoint chunks of $e$ radix-$q$ digits, preceded by a row field whose length is divisible by $W^k$, can be interchanged in $O(Ve^\tau)$ time on fixed tapes.
How: Split rows cyclically among the $W$ roles of the bit network, use shear frames $\Phi_M$ so the network computes $H\gets H+D$ on every role, implement each edge by recursive smaller interchanges, finish the interchange with two linear updates, and solve the volume-normalized recurrence.
Why: Each edge of rank $a$ costs $a$ smaller interchanges on streams of volume $V/W$, and the ranks sum to $s<Wm$, so multiplying the width by $m$ multiplies normalized cost by only $s/W<m^\tau$. (the sources say so: "An edge matrix of rank $a$ will require $a$ smaller interchanges.
Because the sum of these ranks is less than the number of wires times
$m$, the resulting recurrence saves a power of the chunk width." (paper))

Its sub-steps, in order:

- Assigning the projection $P_U$ to each vertex with label $U$ (and $-P_{U_a}$ to source $X_a$) gives fixed rational matrices with $M_{\mathrm{sink}(\rho(w))}-M_{\mathrm{source}(w)}=I_m$ on every role and edge ranks summing to $s_{\rm b}=W_{\rm b}m-N+2L_{\rm b}<W_{\rm b}m$. (on [Rational matrix interface of the bit network](bit-interface.md))
- The relative rank deficits exceed $20\cdot2^{-50}$, so $s_{\rm b}/W_{\rm b}<m^\tau$ (and $s_{\rm c}/W_{\rm c}<m^\sigma$) with $\tau=\sigma=1-2^{-50}$.
- For a fixed rational $m\times m$ matrix $A$ of rank $a$, the shear $(H,D)\mapsto(H+AD,D)$ over $\mathbb Z/q^b\mathbb Z$ takes exactly $a$ interchanges of $b$-digit fields plus $O(V)$ work. (on [Matrix shear by rank-many interchanges](matrix-shear.md))
- On the row-split role streams with frames $\Phi_{M_v}$, the common-frame identity and $M_{\rm out}-M_{\rm in}=I_m$ give $H\gets H+D$ on every original row, including scratch rows with arbitrary initial values.
- The updates $D\gets D-H$, $H\gets H+D$, $D\gets H-D$ send $(H,D)$ to $(D,H)$, and only the middle shear uses the network.
- With $F_k$ the time divided by volume, $F_k\le(s/W)F_{k-1}+O(1)$, and $s/W<m^\tau$ gives $F_k=O(e^\tau)$.
- The recursion runs on one fixed set of tapes with only $O(V)$ extra work per node, for every depth and every spectator length. (on [Fixed-tape depth-first schedule](fixed-tape-schedule.md))

- "The chunks can be interchanged in $O(Ve^\tau)$ time, preserving every other address field, on a fixed number of finite-alphabet tapes." (paper)

  > \begin{proposition}[Power-width interchange with rows] \label{prop:power-interchange} Assume the fixed circuit data satisfy \eqref{eq:finite-shear-contract}, and let $q$ be a prime as in Lemma~\ref{lem:matrix-shear} for its edge matrices, also avoiding the denominators of every assigned terminal and gate matrix $M$. Choose a rational number $\tau\in(0,1)$ with $s/W<m^\tau$. For every $e=m^k$, $k\ge0$, consider two disjoint contiguous address chunks of $e$ radix-$q$ digits each. Suppose a row field preceding both chunks has length divisible by $W^k$. There may be additional fields before and after the row field, between the chunks, and after them. The chunks can be interchanged in $O(Ve^\tau)$ time, preserving every other address field, on a fixed number of finite-alphabet tapes. The constant is independent of all field lengths and of the data. \end{proposition}
