Binary phase frames attach a basis of arrays to each label of [Nested subspace labels and rank deficit](subspace-labels.md), so that moving along a network edge is a product of two-point kernels, one per dimension of change; each edge then costs smaller butterfly-type calls, as the recursion needs.

Labels here are subspaces of $\mathbb F_2^m$, where $m$ is the label-space dimension; the letters $u,v,w,z$ denote vectors of $\mathbb F_2^m$ on this page. Write $\operatorname{wt}(x)$ for the Hamming weight, $[b]\in\{0,1\}$ for the integer representative of $b\in\mathbb F_2$, $P_U$ for the projection onto a label $U$ over $\mathbb F_2$, and $z\cdot w$ for the dot product over $\mathbb F_2$, so $z\cdot z\equiv\operatorname{wt}(z)\pmod 2$. For a label $U$ put $q_U(x)=\operatorname{wt}(P_Ux)\bmod 4$. With $H=\tfrac1{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}$, the phase frame is $\mathcal C_U=H^{\otimes m}\operatorname{diag}_x(i^{q_U(x)})H^{\otimes m}$, acting on arrays indexed by $x\in\mathbb F_2^m$, with Gaussian-dyadic entries. <cite data-source="paper">Each entry of $\mathcal C_U$ and its inverse is a Gaussian integer divided by $2^m$</cite> Let $C=H\operatorname{diag}(1,i)H=aI+bX$ with $a=\frac{1+i}{2}$, $b=\frac{1-i}{2}$, and let $X_v$ be the translation $(X_vf)(x)=f(x+v)$, which flips the coordinates in $\operatorname{supp}(v)$.

## One edge

For binary vectors $z,w$, $\operatorname{wt}(z+w)=\operatorname{wt}(z)+\operatorname{wt}(w)-2|\operatorname{supp}(z)\cap\operatorname{supp}(w)|$, since coordinates where both are $1$ cancel. If $z\cdot w=0$ the intersection cardinality is even, so weight is additive modulo four on orthogonal vectors, and so on pairwise orthogonal families.

Take an edge from $U$ to $V=U\perp E$, where the residual $E$ is orthogonal to $U$ and has an orthonormal basis $\mathcal B$ (distinct vectors orthogonal, $v\cdot v=1$). Then $P_Vx=P_Ux+\sum_{v\in\mathcal B}[v\cdot x]\,v$ with orthogonal summands, so $q_V(x)-q_U(x)=\sum_{v\in\mathcal B}\operatorname{wt}(v)[v\cdot x]\pmod 4$, negated on a decreasing edge, and each $\operatorname{wt}(v)$ is odd. Each term is thus $\varepsilon[v\cdot x]$ with $\varepsilon=\pm1$ modulo four, the sign including the edge direction. The function $i^{\varepsilon[v\cdot x]}$ equals $\frac{1+i^\varepsilon}2+\frac{1-i^\varepsilon}2(-1)^{v\cdot x}$, as one checks for $v\cdot x=0,1$.

Since $H^2=I$, $\mathcal C_V\mathcal C_U^{-1}=H^{\otimes m}\operatorname{diag}(i^{q_V-q_U})H^{\otimes m}$, and the diagonal phases multiply over $v$. The character $(-1)^{v\cdot x}$ is $\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$ on the coordinates in $\operatorname{supp}(v)$. Since $H\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)H=X$, the character $(-1)^{v\cdot x}$ becomes the translation $X_v$, so the phase becomes $aI+bX_v$ or its inverse (for $\varepsilon=-1$ the coefficients swap, and $a^2+b^2=0$, $2ab=1$). Each edge factor is a translate $aI+bX_v$ of [Two-point kernel C](phase-kernel.md), combining only $f(x)$ and $f(x+v)$.

## Counting

A residual of dimension $d$, the number of vectors in $\mathcal B$, costs $d$ factors. Let $W_{\rm c}$ be the wire count of the complex network, $N$ the bank size, and $L_{\rm c}$ the total dimension lost on decreasing edges of that network. Along each wire the edge dimension changes sum to sink minus source dimension, plus twice the losses. Each data wire gains $m-1$ and each scratch wire $m$, a net $W_{\rm c}m-2N$. Hence the number of translation-kernel factors is $s_{\rm c}=W_{\rm c}m-2N+2L_{\rm c}=1873807244636671267308000000<W_{\rm c}m$ <cite data-source="paper">s_{\rm c}=W_{\rm c}m-2N+2L_{\rm c}</cite>, because $L_{\rm c}/N=101/539<1/2$. The deficit of $s_{\rm c}$ from $W_{\rm c}m$ is $2N-2L_{\rm c}>0$.

## Endpoints and the exceptional pair

The roles (wire names) are scratch roles and two data banks $X_a,Y_a$, indexed by tuples $a$ (the subscript is an index, not the coefficient $a$). Source and sink labels are $U_a\to\mathcal F$ on $X_a$, $0\to U_a^\perp$ on $Y_a$, and $0\to\mathcal F$ on scratch. Here $U_a$ is the line spanned by $u_a=t_{a_1}\otimes t_{a_2}\otimes t_{a_3}$, each $t_{a_j}$ the indicator of a three-element set. With $k$ columns (an address is a $k$-tuple of addresses), [Frames on wire arrays](array-frames.md) gives each role the $k$th tensor power of sink frame times inverse source frame, the scalar network exchanging $X_a$ and $Y_a$.

The label $0$ has frame $I$. For $\mathcal F$, $q_{\mathcal F}(x)=\operatorname{wt}(x)$ and $i^{\operatorname{wt}(x)}=\prod_ji^{x_j}$, so the frame is $(H\operatorname{diag}(1,i)H)^{\otimes m}=C^{\otimes m}$. Scratch roles and the route $Y_a\to X_a$ get $C^{\otimes m}$ at once. Only $X_a\to Y_a$, from $U_a$ to $U_a^\perp$, differs.

Take $u=u_a$, the generator of $U_a$. It has $3^3=27$ ones, so $u\cdot u=1$, and $P_{U_a}x=[u\cdot x]u$. Splitting $x=P_{U_a}x+P_{U_a^\perp}x$ into orthogonal parts gives $\operatorname{wt}(x)\equiv q_{U_a}+q_{U_a^\perp}$, hence $q_{U_a^\perp}-q_{U_a}=\operatorname{wt}(x)-2\operatorname{wt}(u)[u\cdot x]=\operatorname{wt}(x)+2[u\cdot x]\pmod 4$. As $2[u\cdot x]\equiv2\sum_{j\in\operatorname{supp}(u)}x_j$, the phase per coordinate is $i^{3x_j}=i^{-x_j}$ on the 27 coordinates of $\operatorname{supp}(u)$ and $i^{x_j}$ elsewhere: the operator is $C^{-1}$ on those 27 coordinates and $C$ elsewhere.

Put $Z=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$. Since $ZXZ=-X$, $C^{-1}=bI+aX$, $ib=a$ and $ia=-b$, we get $C=iZC^{-1}Z$. Let $Z_{a,k}$ be $Z$ on those 27 coordinates in every column. Apply $Z_{a,k}$ before the network at $X_a$ and $i^{27k}Z_{a,k}$ after it at $Y_a$. Each of the $27k$ inverse factors then becomes $C$, so this route is $C^{\otimes mk}$ as well. Only the physical $X$ outputs still carry the scalar exchange's minus sign: negate them and return each output to its input's role name. Every role, scratch included, then receives $C^{\otimes mk}$.
