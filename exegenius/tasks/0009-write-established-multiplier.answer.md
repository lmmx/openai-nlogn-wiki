The established multiplier, also called the Harvey–van der Hoeven multiplier, is a known algorithm for [exact integer multiplication](exact-integer-multiplication.md): it takes two binary strings and outputs their product. Here $n$ is the number of bits in each input string, so the product has $2n$ bits. The algorithm runs in $O(n\log n)$ time on a fixed-tape machine, a Turing machine whose number of tapes does not grow with the input.

The argument needs it only as a fast product that can be treated as a black box. Its internals are not opened: the polynomial products of [signed coefficient packing](signed-packing.md) call it as an ordinary subroutine. The paper therefore does not apply its improved result to the multiplier itself, since <cite data-source="paper">they do not invoke the improved theorem recursively</cite>.
