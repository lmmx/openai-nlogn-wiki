# E9: the parameters-and-cases sentence, three argument runs on E6's extractions

Records of experiment E9, run in the development session of lmmx/exegenius. The plan, criteria and ledger are in `calibration/plans/E9.md`, committed before the runs. The results are in that plan and in `docs/journal/2026-10-14-e8b-e9.md`.

This branch starts from `141026-exp-s4-e8-fb1`, so it also holds the comparisons: E8FkX, and E7FkW.

**The runs:**
- `runs/E9F2Y`, `E9F4Y` and `E9F8Y` are copies of E6's projects F2, F4 and F8 (background accepted).
- Their argument task was rendered by exegenius branch `141026-exp-params-cases` (e8efaa3), the E8 instruction plus the parameters-and-cases sentence.
- Each task file was checked by diff against E8FkX's, apart from the path and the sentence.
- Each prompt went word for word to a fresh `general-purpose` subagent with `model: "sonnet"` and `effort: "high"`.
- The three ran in parallel from 18:45 UTC, and all were accepted at their first submit. None stopped on `max_tokens`.

**E9F8Y after acceptance:**
- Its subagent then rewrote its answer file and tried a second `submit`, which was refused as not pending.
- The accepted plan is `exegenius/argument.json`. Two of its strings carry control characters from unescaped backslashes.

**Transcripts:** `results/transcripts/e9/subagents/` holds the three argument subagents' transcripts and `.meta.json`, with descriptions such as `E9F2Y 0003-argument`.
