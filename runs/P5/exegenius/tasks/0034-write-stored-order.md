## This page

Concept: Retained bit-reversed frequency order
Gloss: The forward synthetic transform writes outputs in bit-reversed slots; the pointwise product and the opposite-sign transform consume that same order, so no reordering is executed.

## This page in the argument

The step: The forward transform writes entry $j$ at slot $\operatorname{rev}_a(j)$; the pointwise product and the opposite transform use this same order, so no reordering is executed.

It serves these steps, from the main result down:

- A deterministic fixed-tape Turing machine multiplies $n$-bit integers exactly in $O(n(\lg n)^{1-\kappa})$ time with $\kappa=2^{-182}$.; how: build two tape procedures that save a power of the width, use them inside the Harvey and van der Hoeven Gaussian-resampling multiplication with polynomial transforms and retained permutations, then choose parameters, prove exactness and sum the costs (on [Integer multiplication in O(n (lg n)^{1−κ}) time](main-theorem.md))
- Synthetic polynomial transforms and ring convolution run in $O(Tp[dK+pK^{\tau-1}+\ell d^{\lambda'}+\log(rp)])$ time.; how: implement the Fourier transforms over the ring where roots of unity are signed shifts, using the simultaneous layers, then multiply pointwise
- A forward synthetic transform over $\mathbb C[y]/(y^r+1)$, laid out so each radix-two round is one simultaneous layer, costs $O(Tp[dK+pK^{\tau-1}+\ell d^{\lambda'}])$ and leaves its output in bit-reversed order.; how: write the transform as rounds of butterflies and signed-shift twiddles, then place the selected bits of the participating axes at a common offset so each round is a simultaneous layer (on [Synthetic transform layout and cost](transform-layout.md))

## Evidence from the sources, with the paragraph around each quote

- "The two forward synthetic transforms retain the same frequency order through their pointwise product" (paper)

  > Next, Bluestein's chirp identity converts the middle transform, including its target permutation, into normalized cyclic convolution. A phase twist changes the last cyclic coordinate into a polynomial modulo $y^r+1$; its negative wrap gives precisely the original cyclic wrap after the twist is removed. Sections~\ref{sec:synthetic-layouts} and~\ref{sec:exact-ring-products} compute convolution on the remaining axes over this polynomial ring. The two forward synthetic transforms retain the same frequency order through their pointwise product, and the opposite transform consumes that order. Their polynomial products use signed packing and the established $O(n\log n)$ multiplier as an ordinary subroutine.

- "Let $B_t$ denote this record permutation: it moves the entry at $j$" (paper)

  > After all $a$ rounds, the named slots from high to low therefore contain the frequency bits from low to high. If $\operatorname{rev}_a(j)$ reverses the $a$ binary digits of $j$, the entry $(F_t^-z)_j$ occupies slot $\operatorname{rev}_a(j)$. Let $B_t$ denote this record permutation: it moves the entry at $j$ to $\operatorname{rev}_a(j)$. The recursion writes its outputs directly in this order; it does not move whole frequency indices.
